---
name: Show the working
description: Use for any content with worked examples or answers, so every solution shows its working line by line and is correct.
revision: 1
---
- Work every example line by line. Write each line of working in LaTeX between $ signs, and
  give it a short commentary that says why it follows. Never give commentary without its
  working.
- Work examples and classwork on what the objective names: a step on algebraic terms works on
  algebraic terms, not on bare numbers.
- Every worked example and every answer must be mathematically correct. Check each one before
  you write it.
