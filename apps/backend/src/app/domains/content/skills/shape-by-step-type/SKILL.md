---
name: Shape by step type
description: Use for content written one plan step at a time, so each step's content has the shape its kind of step needs.
revision: 1
---
Shape the content to the step, as the plan types it with step_kind, kc_type and
instructional_type. Each kind of step carries its own events of instruction.

- **Intro** (step_kind intro): recall what the lesson builds on. Give a numbered list of the
  earlier topics, then a few quick questions on them. Teach nothing new.
- **Concept** (kc_type concept): state definitions, properties and facts, with lists, tables or
  labelled figures, and instances of the idea. Give no worked calculation unless the objective
  asks for one.
- **Procedure** (kc_type procedure): state the rule briefly, then work at least three graded
  examples, from simple to harder, each with parts where that helps.
- **Evaluation** (instructional_type check): questions with their answers only.

Teach only the step's objective. Use nothing a later step of the lesson teaches, and nothing
from a later lesson. Do not restate the objective or the step's title: the plan carries them.
