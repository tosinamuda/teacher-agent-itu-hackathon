---
name: Figures from the plan
description: Use for content that may carry drawings, so every drawing is correct and none is referred to without being drawn.
revision: 1
---
- A figure the plan step has is copied into the figures exactly as the plan draws it.
- Draw a new figure only for a plain shape or angle. Never draw a construction the plan does not
  draw: a construction drawn by guesswork is wrong more often than right.
- A drawing is one tikzpicture environment whose labels match the text.
- Never refer to a diagram, figure or picture ("as shown", "in the diagram") that the content
  does not draw.
- A construction gives its procedure as numbered steps that work with ruler and compasses as
  written.
