---
name: Build on the plan
description: Use for content made from a lesson plan, so it keeps and expands the plan's own teaching instead of inventing it again.
revision: 1
---
Each plan step may carry blocks: explanations, worked examples with their working and figures,
and practice. They are the plan's own teaching.

- Keep what the blocks teach, in their order, correct as they are. Expand around them.
- Keep a procedure the plan gives, step for step; never replace it with one of your own.
- Where a step has no blocks, write from its title, objective and activities.
- Keep the evaluation step's questions for the evaluation: never use them as examples or
  classwork in another step.
