## Why the thin rows are thin
`student_tasks` carries the work a core step sets. Her questions come first: across the cohort the core steps carry 57 blocks in total, `explanation` 31, `practice` 4, `worked_example` 22, so 4 units can carry the field from a block.

Where a step has no question text the Student Activity column is read instead, and only where her Gagne coder reads it as performance — a construction she set has no question to carry it. That is 5 further units. Reception ("listen", "take notes") is coded as nothing and crosses nowhere, and no teacher activity crosses at all.

`assessment_points` reads the questions on an `evaluation` step. t3-w06 has none, which is why that row is empty there.
