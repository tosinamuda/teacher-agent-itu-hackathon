#### Perimeter

Perimeter is the distance round a shape.

**Worked examples**

##### 1. Calculate the perimeter of a square of side 15cm.

**fig-05.** A square, named *Square* at its left, with all four sides marked twice — $L$ and
15cm.

\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.42]
  \draw (0,0) rectangle (5,5);
  \node[left]  at (-1.4,4.4) {Square};
  \node[above] at (2.5,5) {$L$\quad 15cm};
  \node[below] at (2.5,0) {$L$\quad 15cm};
  \node[left]  at (0,3) {15cm};
  \node[left]  at (0,1.8) {$L$};
  \node[right] at (5,3) {15cm};
  \node[right] at (5,1.8) {$L$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}

**Solution**

Perimeter of a square $= L + L + L + L = 4L$

$= 15 + 15 + 15 + 15 = 4(15)$

$= 60\mathrm{cm}$

##### 2. Find the perimeter of a rectangle whose length 10cm and breadth 7cm.

**fig-06.** A rectangle with both long sides marked $L$, the top one also 10cm, and both short
sides marked $b$, the right-hand one also 7cm.

\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.42]
  \draw (0,0) rectangle (7,4.5);
  \node[above] at (3.5,4.5) {$L$\quad 10cm};
  \node[below] at (3.5,0) {$L$};
  \node[left]  at (0,2.25) {$b$};
  \node[right] at (7,2.7) {$b$};
  \node[right] at (7,1.6) {7cm};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}

**Solution**

Perimeter of rectangle $= L + b + L + b$

$= L + L + b + b$

$= 2L + 2b$

$= 2(L + B)$

$= 10\mathrm{cm} + 7\mathrm{cm} + 10\mathrm{cm} + 7\mathrm{cm}$

$= 10\mathrm{cm} + 10\mathrm{cm} + 7\mathrm{cm} + 7\mathrm{cm}$

$= 2(10) + 2(7)$

$= 2(10 + 7)$

$= 2(17)$

$= 34\mathrm{cm}$

##### 3. If the perimeter of a rectangle is 60cm and its length is 17cm. Calculate its breadth.

**fig-07.** A rectangle with both long sides marked $L = 17$cm and both short sides marked $b$.

\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.42]
  \draw (0,0) rectangle (7,4);
  \node[above] at (3.5,4) {$L = 17$cm};
  \node[below] at (3.5,0) {$L = 17$cm};
  \node[left]  at (0,2) {$b$};
  \node[right] at (7,2) {$b$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}

**Solution**

Perimeter $= 60\mathrm{cm}$

$P = L + b + L + b$

$60 = 17\mathrm{cm} + b + 17\mathrm{cm} + b$

$60 = 17\mathrm{cm} + 17\mathrm{cm} + b + b$

$60\mathrm{cm} = 2(17\mathrm{cm}) + 2b$

$60\mathrm{cm} = 2(17\mathrm{cm} + b)$

$60\mathrm{cm} = 34\mathrm{cm} + 2b$

collect like terms.

$60\mathrm{cm} - 34\mathrm{cm} = 2b$

$2b = 26\mathrm{cm}$

Divide both side by 2

$\frac{2b}{2} = \frac{26\mathrm{cm}}{2}$

$b = 13\mathrm{cm}$

##### 4. Find the perimeter of the trapezium below.

**fig-08.** A trapezium with each side lettered and measured: the top $c$, 5cm; the left $b$,
9cm; the right $d$, 4cm; the base $a$, 11cm.

\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.42]
  \draw (0,0)--(7.5,0)--(6.0,3.2)--(2.0,3.2)--cycle;
  \node[below] at (3.75,0) {$a$\quad 11cm};
  \node[above] at (4.0,3.2) {$c$\quad 5cm};
  \node[left]  at (0.95,2.3) {9cm};
  \node[left]  at (0.65,1.35) {$b$};
  \node[right] at (6.9,2.3) {4cm};
  \node[right] at (7.15,1.35) {$d$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}

Perimeter of trapezium $= a + b + c + d$

$= 11\mathrm{cm} + 9\mathrm{cm} + 5\mathrm{cm} + 4\mathrm{cm}$

$= 29\mathrm{cm}$

##### 5. Find the perimeter of the parallelogram below.

**fig-09.** A parallelogram with a single arrow on each of its four sides: the two horizontal
ones both lettered $a$, the lower also measured 9cm, and the two slanted ones both lettered $b$,
the right-hand one also measured 5cm.

\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.42]
  \draw (0,0)--(6,0)--(8,3.2)--(2,3.2)--cycle;
  \draw[->] (2.7,0)--(2.9,0);
  \draw[->] (4.7,3.2)--(4.9,3.2);
  \draw[->] (0.9,1.5)--(1.05,1.75);
  \draw[->] (6.9,1.5)--(7.05,1.75);
  \node[below] at (3,0) {9cm\quad $a$};
  \node[above] at (5,3.2) {$a$};
  \node[left]  at (0.9,1.6) {$b$};
  \node[right] at (7.2,1.9) {$b$};
  \node[right] at (7.4,1.1) {5cm};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}

**Solution**

Perimeter of parallelogram $= a + a + b + b$

$= 2a + 2b$

$= 2(a + b)$

$= 2(9 + 5)$

$= 2(14)$

$= 28\mathrm{cm}$

##### 6. Find the perimeter of a regular hexagon sides 5.2cm.

**Solution.**

**fig-10.** A regular hexagon, named above the drawing, with each of its six sides marked 5.2cm.

\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.42]
  \foreach \i in {0,...,5} { \coordinate (H\i) at ({\i*60}:3); }
  \draw (H0)--(H1)--(H2)--(H3)--(H4)--(H5)--cycle;
  \node[above]       at (0,4.0) {Regular hexagon};
  \node[above]       at (0,2.65) {5.2cm};
  \node[above right] at (2.4,1.3) {5.2cm};
  \node[below right] at (2.4,-1.3) {5.2cm};
  \node[below]       at (0,-2.65) {5.2cm};
  \node[below left]  at (-2.4,-1.3) {5.2cm};
  \node[above left]  at (-2.4,1.3) {5.2cm};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}

Hexagon has 6 sides.

Perimeter $= 6 \times 5.2\mathrm{cm}$

$= 31.2\mathrm{cm}$

##### 7. Calculate the perimeter of circle of radius 7cm (use the value $3\frac{1}{7}$ for $\pi$)

**Solution**

**fig-11.** A circle with a radius drawn from the centre to the right of the circumference,
marked 7cm.

\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.42]
  \draw (0,0) circle (3);
  \draw (0,0)--(3,0) node[midway,above] {7cm};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}

Perimeter of a circle $= 2\pi r$ or $\pi d$

$= 2 \times 3\frac{1}{7} \times 7\mathrm{cm}$

$= 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 7$

$= 2 \times 22 = 44\mathrm{cm}$

using $\pi d$.

Since $r = 7\mathrm{cm}$

$d = 2 \times r = 2 \times 7\mathrm{cm} = 14\mathrm{cm}$

$\frac{22}{7} \times 14\mathrm{cm}$

$= 22 \times 2$

$= 44\mathrm{cm}$

##### 8. A rope is wound 50 times round a cylinder of radius 25cm. How long is the rope? $\pi = 3.14$.

**Solution**

**fig-12.** An upright cylinder with a rope wound round it, drawn as a series of turns spiralling
down the body from the top face to the base.

\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.42]
  \draw (0,0) ellipse (2 and 0.6);
  \draw (-2,0)--(-2,-6);
  \draw (2,0)--(2,-6);
  \draw (-2,-6) arc[start angle=180, end angle=360, x radius=2, y radius=0.6];
  \foreach \y in {-0.9,-1.8,-2.7,-3.6,-4.5,-5.4} {
    \draw (-2,\y) arc[start angle=180, end angle=360, x radius=2, y radius=0.55];
    \draw (2,\y-0.03)--(-2,\y-0.9);
  }
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}

length of the rope $=$ circumference.

Circumference $= 2\pi r$

$= 2 \times 3.14 \times 25$

$= 157\mathrm{cm}$

Total length of the rope $= 157\mathrm{cm} \times 50$

$= 7850\mathrm{cm}$.

##### 9. The circumference of a circle is 440cm. Find the radius $(\pi = \frac{22}{7})$

**Solution**

**fig-13.** A circle with a radius drawn from the centre to the right of the circumference,
marked $r$.

\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.42]
  \draw (0,0) circle (3);
  \draw (0,0)--(3,0) node[midway,above] {$r$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}

Circumference $= 2\pi r$

$440 = 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times r$

$440 = \frac{44r}{7}$

cross multiply.

$440 \times 7 = 44r$

$3080 = 44r$

divide both side by $44$

$\frac{44r}{44} = \frac{3080}{44}$

$r = 70\mathrm{cm}$

##### 10. Calculate the perimeter of the shape below.

**fig-14.** A quarter circle: an upright side and a base meeting at a right angle, each marked
4.9cm, with an arc closing them from the top of the upright to the end of the base.

\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.42]
  \draw (0,0)--(0,5)--(0,5) arc[start angle=90, end angle=0, radius=5]--(0,0);
  \node[left]  at (0,2.5) {4.9 cm};
  \node[below] at (2.5,0) {4.9cm};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}

**Solution**

length of the curved part $= \frac{1}{4} \times 2\pi r$

$= \frac{1}{4} \times 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 4.9$

$= \frac{1}{4} \times \frac{44}{7} \times 4.9$

$= \frac{11}{7} \times \frac{49}{10}$

$= \frac{11 \times 7}{10}$

$= \frac{77}{10}$

$= 7.7\mathrm{cm}$

Perimeter of the shape $= 4.9 + 4.9 + 7.7$

$= 17.5\mathrm{cm}$
