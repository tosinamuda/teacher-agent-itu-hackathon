#### Example 1. Find the value of the following

(a) $18 - 6 \times 2$

(b) $6 - 18 \div 3$

(c) $6 \times 2 - 2 - 40 \div 4$

**Solution**

(a) $18 - 6 \times 2$

$= 18 - 12$

$= 6$

(b) $6 - 18 \div 3$

$= 6 - 6$

$= 0$

(c) $(6 \times 2) - 2 - (40 \div 4)$

$= 12 - 2 - 10$

$= 10 - 10$

$= 0$

#### Example 2. Simplify the following

(a) $3x \times 2 + 5x$

(b) $17y - 5 \times 2y$

**Solution**

(a) $3x \times 2 + 5x$

$= 6x + 5x$

$= 11x$

(b) $17y - 5 \times 2y$

$= 17y - 10y$

$= 7y$

#### Example 3. Simplify the following

(a) $7x \div x + 5$

(b) $4x - (6 \div 3)$

(c) $(24x \div 6) + x + (1 \times 5x)$

**Solution**

(a) $7x \div x + 5$

$= \frac{7x}{x} + 5$

$= 7 + 5$

$= 12$

(b) $4x - (6 \div 3)$

$= 4x - 2$

(c) $(24x \div 6) + x + (1 \times 5x)$

$= 4x + x + 5x$

$= 5x + 5x$

$= 10x$
