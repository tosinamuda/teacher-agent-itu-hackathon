#### Volume of cube and cuboid

1. Volume of cuboid = Area of base $\times$ height

   $= \text{length} \times \text{breadth} \times \text{height}$

   $= lbh$

2. Volume of cube = Length $\times$ Length $\times$ Length

   $= l^{3}$

**Example 1.** Calculate the volume of a cuboid of length 3cm, breadth 2cm and height 8cm.

Solution:

Volume of cuboid $= L \times b \times h$

length $= 3cm$

breadth $= 2cm$

height $= 8cm$

Volume $= 3cm \times 2cm \times 8cm$

$= 48cm^{3}$

**Example 2.** The volume of a cuboid is 24cm$^{3}$. If the base is 6cm by 2cm. Find the
height of the cuboid.

Solution:

Volume of cuboid $= L \times b \times h$

length $= 6cm$

breadth $= 2cm$

height $= ?$

Volume $= 24cm^{3}$

$24cm^{3} = 6cm \times 2cm \times h$

$24cm^{3} = 12cm^{2}h$

Divide both side by 12cm$^{2}$

$\frac{24cm^{3}}{12cm^{2}} = \frac{12cm^{2}h}{12cm^{2}}$

$2cm = h$

$h = 2cm$

#### Volume of cube

**Example 3.** Calculate the volume of a cube of side 3cm.

Solution

Volume of cube $= l^{3}$

$V = (3cm)^{3}$

$V = 3cm \times 3cm \times 3cm$

$V = 27cm^{3}$

#### Volume of prism

Calculate the volume of the prism below.

**fig-03.** A triangular prism lying on its rectangular face. The triangular end has a base
of 5 m and a height of 3 m, and the prism runs back 10 m.

\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.42,line width=0.5pt]
  \draw (0,0) -- (5,0) -- (2.5,3) -- cycle;
  \draw (5,0) -- (8.5,1.8);
  \draw (2.5,3) -- (6,4.8);
  \draw (8.5,1.8) -- (6,4.8);
  \draw[dashed] (0,0) -- (3.5,1.8);
  \draw[dashed] (3.5,1.8) -- (8.5,1.8);
  \draw[dashed] (3.5,1.8) -- (6,4.8);
  \draw[dashed] (2.5,0) -- (2.5,3);
  \node[below] at (2.5,-0.15) {$5\,\mathrm{m}$};
  \node[left] at (2.35,1.5) {$3\,\mathrm{m}$};
  \node[below right] at (6.9,0.8) {$10\,\mathrm{m}$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}

Solution:

Area of triangular face $= \frac{1}{2}bh$

$= \frac{1}{2} \times 5 \times 3$

$= 7.5m^{2}$

Volume of prism $=$ Area of base $\times$ height

$= 7.5m^{2} \times 10m$

$= 75m^{3}$
