#### Three Dimensional shapes

These are shapes that have length, breadth and height. They are called solid shapes.

They may be either regular or irregular shapes. Regular shapes are cube of sugar, can drinks,
match box, etc. Irregular shapes are stone, trees etc.

#### Properties of cube

**fig-01.** A cube drawn as a see-through solid: two equal squares, the nearer one offset down and to the
right of the farther one, with the four pairs of corresponding corners joined.

\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.5]
  \draw (0,1.1) rectangle (4,5.1);
  \draw (1.1,0) rectangle (5.1,4);
  \draw (0,1.1)--(1.1,0);
  \draw (4,1.1)--(5.1,0);
  \draw (4,5.1)--(5.1,4);
  \draw (0,5.1)--(1.1,4);
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}

1. It is a solid state
2. It has 6 square faces
3. It has 12 edges
4. It has 8 vertices.

**Hint.** An edge is a line where two faces meet. It may be straight or curved. A face or
surface may be flat or curved. A vertex is a point corner where edges meet.

#### Properties of a Cuboid

**fig-02.** A cuboid drawn the same way, wide and shallow, with three leader lines to the right naming the
top face, the nearer top-right corner and the nearer right-hand vertical edge.

\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.5]
