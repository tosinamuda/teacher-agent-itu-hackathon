# What C0 and C1 actually send the model

Generated by `dump_inputs.py` from the frozen eval set, through `prompt_lesson` and
`prompt_unit` — the same two functions the generator calls — so what is printed below is
the payload, not a second rendering of it. Dumping the models directly is how
`entry_behaviour: []` stayed invisible in this file while C0 was reading it.

**C0** — the curriculum specification: objectives, subject, grade, topic, subtopic.
**C1** — C0 plus the lesson plan's design fields, enumerated in `C1-DEFINITION.md`.

Sections are generated one unit at a time by independent concurrent calls, so each
`unit_context` below is one LM call, and every call in a lesson sees the same
`lesson_context`. A field with no source in a given plan is absent for that lesson;
nothing is supplied to fill it, which is why the shapes differ between lessons.

---

## The task contract

Chosen by what the profile provides, never by condition label. Both carry the SAME
instructional-events codebook: dspy replaces a parent docstring rather than extending it,
so a codebook on the step-level signature alone would put instructional-design prose in
C1's prompt and none in C0's — and that prose, rather than the lesson plan, could then
explain a C1 advantage. C0 receives the legend and never receives the field.

### C0 — `WriteObjectiveSection`

```text
Write one revision-pack section teaching the given lesson objective.

Write for the audience in artefact_spec, following its every requirement.
Write your own reader-facing title and never copy a planning term into the
section. Every practice question ends with its answer. Preserve the lesson
objective exactly.

lesson_context and unit_context may carry more than the objective — what the
learner already knows, how the lesson taught this material, what it asked
them to do and what it assessed. Read those fields as the contract below
defines them.

LESSON-PLAN CONTEXT. The lesson objective is what this section must teach, and
it sets the section's scope. The fields below describe how the teacher planned
to teach, practise and assess that objective. Any of them may be absent, and
absence removes nothing artefact_spec requires.

entry_behaviour
  What the learner is expected to know already. Connect the objective to it
  where that helps; never teach it as new material.

current_step
  This objective's position in the ordered lesson_objectives. Keep to that
  progression — assume what precedes it, leave what follows to its own section
  — and never narrate the ordering to the reader.

instructional_materials
  Resources chosen for this lesson. Use one where it genuinely helps someone
  work through this objective alone; ignore it where it does not.

student_tasks
  Practice the lesson set for this objective. Keep its intent; the section
  still teaches the objective in full.

assessment_points
  Questions the lesson used to check this objective, for the learner to
  self-check against. Where one reaches past the objective, it does not narrow
  or replace what this section teaches.

activities
  What the teacher and the students did for this objective, in the order the
  plan records it, in the teacher's own words. Read it for the representation
  she chose, the order she taught in, what the students were asked to do and
  how their work was checked, and keep to those. A student line marked
  sets_work is work the lesson set; one not so marked is participation inside
  the teacher's explanation, not a task. Where a line says the same thing as
  student_tasks, student_tasks is the statement of the task — do not set it
  twice. Never narrate any of this: the reader meets the mathematics, not the
  classroom, and is never told what the teacher or the class did.

INSTRUCTIONAL EVENTS. Where unit_context gives instructional_events, it names
the teaching functions this section's material was taught through, in order.
Where activities is also given, the events are its labels and the activities
are what they label; read the events through the activities, not instead of
them.
Realise them for a learner working alone, following that order where it works
on the page:

  stimulate_recall           bring back the named prior learning briefly,
                             before the new material
  present_content            explain, show or set out the new material
  provide_learning_guidance  support the learner through it before asking them
                             to do it alone — a worked solution, a demonstration,
                             a scaffold or a cue, whichever suits the objective
  elicit_performance         have the learner attempt the task themselves
  provide_feedback           let the learner check and correct their own work

The list is what this lesson planned, so it may name only some of these and may
name one more than once. Give those functions the room the lesson gave them.
It never licenses less than artefact_spec requires: every requirement there
still holds, whether or not an event names it. Where the list is absent or
empty, write the section as artefact_spec requires.

These names are for you, never for the reader: the section describes the
mathematics, never the teaching.
```

### artefact_spec — identical in both conditions

```json
{
  "artefact": "one section of a take-home revision pack",
  "audience": "a junior secondary student aged about 11-13 who sat in this lesson and is revising alone at home",
  "requirements": [
    "Speak directly to the learner about the mathematics, never about the lesson as an event: no mention of the teacher or what happened in class, no lesson step or objective labels, no mention of where any material came from.",
    "Write your own learner-facing section title; nothing in the inputs is a heading for the reader.",
    "Teach the objective inside this lesson's topic and subtopic: examples and practice must be of the kind the topic names (a lesson on algebraic expressions practises on algebraic terms, not on bare numbers).",
    "Every practice question must have its answer in the section - answers at the end, so the learner attempts first and then checks.",
    "Never refer to a picture, space, partner or answer key that is not in the pack itself."
  ]
}
```
