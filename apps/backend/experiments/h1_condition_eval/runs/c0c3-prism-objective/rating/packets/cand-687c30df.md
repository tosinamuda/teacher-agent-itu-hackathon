## The revision pack

### Carrying Out Order of Operations in Algebra

When you simplify an algebraic expression, the terms must be dealt with in a set order. You already know how to add, subtract, multiply and divide algebraic terms; the order of operations simply tells you *which* operation to do *first* when several appear in one expression.

Work through the operations in this order:

1. **Brackets** — work out anything inside brackets first.
2. **Division and Multiplication** — do these next, working from left to right.
3. **Addition and Subtraction** — do these last, working from left to right.

The important point for algebra is that **multiplication and division are always done before addition and subtraction**. A very common mistake is to add terms first because they look alike — but the order of operations must be respected first.

For example, in `4n + 3n × 10`, you must NOT add `4n + 3n` first, because the multiplication `3n × 10` has to come before the addition. Deal with the multiplication, then add what is left.

**Example 1 — Simplify `4n + 3n × 10`**

Multiplication comes before addition:
`3n × 10 = 30n`

Now add the like terms:
`4n + 30n = 34n`

So `4n + 3n × 10 = 34n`.

**Example 2 — Simplify `32 + 8x ÷ 2`**

Division comes before addition:
`8x ÷ 2 = 4x`

Now add:
`32 + 4x`

`32` and `4x` are not like terms, so they cannot be combined. The answer stays as `32 + 4x`.

**Example 3 — Simplify `3 × 5x + 4x ÷ 2`**

Do the multiplication and the division first, working from left to right:
`3 × 5x = 15x` and `4x ÷ 2 = 2x`

Now add the like terms:
`15x + 2x = 17x`

So `3 × 5x + 4x ÷ 2 = 17x`.

**Example 4 — Simplify `36y ÷ 6 + y + 2 × 4y`**

Do the division and the multiplication first, from left to right:
`36y ÷ 6 = 6y` and `2 × 4y = 8y`

Now add all the like terms:
`6y + y + 8y = 15y`

So `36y ÷ 6 + y + 2 × 4y = 15y`.

### Removing Brackets to Simplify

Brackets in algebra group terms together so we can treat them as one piece. To simplify an expression that contains brackets, you first remove the brackets and then collect like terms. Everything depends on the sign written directly in front of the bracket.

• If a plus sign (+) is in front of the bracket, remove the bracket and leave every sign inside exactly as it is.
• If a minus sign (−) is in front of the bracket, remove the bracket and change every sign inside: + becomes −, and − becomes +.

Only the sign in front of the bracket matters — a number written right against a bracket (like $3(2a+1)$) means multiplication, which is a separate idea. Here, every bracket is added or subtracted as a whole.

You can also work the other way and insert a bracket. If the bracket you put in has a plus sign before it, keep the signs of the grouped terms as they are. If it has a minus sign before it, change the signs inside. For example, $3x - y + 4$ can be written as $3x - (y - 4)$, because removing that bracket gives $3x - y + 4$ again.

Simplify $(12a - 7) + (4 - 5a)$.

There is a plus sign in front of each bracket, so remove both brackets without changing any sign inside:

$$12a - 7 + 4 - 5a$$

Now collect like terms. The terms with $a$ are $12a$ and $-5a$; the constant terms are $-7$ and $+4$.

$$12a - 5a = 7a \quad \text{and} \quad -7 + 4 = -3$$

So the answer is:

$$7a - 3$$

Now a case with a minus sign. Simplify $(8x - 2y) - (9x - 4y)$.

The first bracket has a plus (implied) in front, so it is removed as it is. The second bracket has a minus in front, so every sign inside is changed: $9x$ becomes $-9x$ and $-4y$ becomes $+4y$.

$$8x - 2y - 9x + 4y$$

Collect like terms:

$$8x - 9x = -x \quad \text{and} \quad -2y + 4y = 2y$$

So the answer is:

$$-x + 2y$$

Simplify $12 - (7 - 2f) - (3 - f)$.

The first $12$ has no bracket. The bracket $(7 - 2f)$ has a minus in front, so change its signs: $7$ becomes $-7$ and $-2f$ becomes $+2f$. The bracket $(3 - f)$ also has a minus in front, so $3$ becomes $-3$ and $-f$ becomes $+f$.

$$12 - 7 + 2f - 3 + f$$

Collect like terms. For the constants: $12 - 7 - 3 = 2$. For the $f$-terms: $2f + f = 3f$.

$$3f + 2$$

You can also insert a bracket. Write $3x - y + 4$ with the last two terms inside a bracket that has a minus in front. To keep the signs right when the bracket is removed, put $y - 4$ inside:

$$3x - (y - 4)$$

Check by removing the bracket: the minus changes $y$ to $-y$ and $-4$ to $+4$, giving $3x - y + 4$, which matches the original.

**Practice**

1. Simplify $(8a - 5) + (14 - 3a)$.

2. Simplify $(5m + 8) - (2m + 3)$.

3. Simplify $14 - (3 - 4k) - (2 + k)$.

### Turning Word Problems into Algebraic Expressions

Sometimes a problem is given to you in words rather than as a ready-made expression. Your job is to read the words carefully, work out which quantity each letter stands for, and then build an algebraic expression from what you are told.

The key is to decide two things:
1. What does each letter represent?
2. How do the words describe a relationship between the quantities?

Words like "more than" tell you to add, "less than" tells you to subtract, and "altogether" or "total" tells you to add things together. Work step by step, and write down what each letter stands for before you try to build the expression.

**Worked example**

A pen costs $h$ naira, and a crayon costs ₦20 less than a pen.

**(a) Find the cost of a crayon.**

First, decide what the letter stands for: $h$ is the cost of a pen in naira.

A crayon costs ₦20 *less* than a pen, so we subtract 20 from the cost of the pen:

$$\text{cost of a crayon} = h - 20$$

**(b) Find the cost of a crayon and a pen.**

"and" here means we add the two costs together. The pen costs $h$ naira and the crayon costs $h - 20$ naira:

$$\text{cost of a crayon and a pen} = h + (h - 20)$$

Now simplify by removing the bracket and collecting like terms:

$$h + h - 20 = 2h - 20$$

So a crayon costs $(h - 20)$ naira, and a crayon together with a pen costs $(2h - 20)$ naira.

**Worked example**

A pen and a pencil altogether cost $x$ naira. If a pen costs $y$ naira, find the cost of 3 pencils.

Step 1 — find the cost of one pencil.

"Altogether" means the pen and pencil added together make $x$ naira. The pen costs $y$ naira, so:

$$y + \text{cost of one pencil} = x$$

To get the cost of one pencil, subtract $y$ from both sides:

$$\text{cost of one pencil} = x - y$$

Step 2 — find the cost of 3 pencils.

Three pencils cost three times one pencil:

$$3 \times (x - y)$$

So 3 pencils cost $3(x - y)$ naira, which we could also write as $3x - 3y$.

**Practice**

1. Envelopes cost $d$ naira per pack. Four notepads cost 20 naira more than a pack of envelopes. Find the total cost of a pack of envelopes and one notepad.

2. A book costs $b$ naira and a ruler costs ₦10 less than the book. What is the total cost of 2 books and 3 rulers?

3. A pen and a pencil altogether cost $x$ naira. If a pen costs $y$ naira, find the cost of 3 pencils. (Simplify your answer.)

5. 5a

   Brackets come first: 3a + 5a = 8a. Now the expression is 7 × 3a − 8a × 2. Do the multiplications next, left to right: 7 × 3a = 21a and 8a × 2 = 16a. Then subtract: 21a − 16a = 5a.

**Removing Brackets to Simplify**

1. $5a + 9$

   Both brackets have a plus in front, so remove them without changing signs: $8a - 5 + 14 - 3a$. Collect like terms: $8a - 3a = 5a$ and $-5 + 14 = 9$. Answer: $5a + 9$.

2. $3m + 5$

   The first bracket has a plus in front, so keep its signs: $5m + 8$. The second bracket has a minus in front, so change its signs: $2m$ becomes $-2m$ and $3$ becomes $-3$. This gives $5m + 8 - 2m - 3$. Collect like terms: $5m - 2m = 3m$ and $8 - 3 = 5$. Answer: $3m + 5$.

3. $3k + 9$

   The first bracket $(3 - 4k)$ has a minus in front, so change its signs: $3$ becomes $-3$ and $-4k$ becomes $+4k$. The second bracket $(2 + k)$ has a minus in front, so $2$ becomes $-2$ and $k$ becomes $-k$. This gives $14 - 3 + 4k - 2 - k$. Collect like terms: $14 - 3 - 2 = 9$ and $4k - k = 3k$. Answer: $3k + 9$.

**Turning Word Problems into Algebraic Expressions**

1. A pack of envelopes costs $d$ naira. One notepad costs $\frac{d+20}{4}$ naira. Total cost of a pack of envelopes and one notepad is $d + \frac{d+20}{4}$ naira.

   Four notepads cost 20 naira more than a pack of envelopes, which is $d + 20$ naira. So one notepad costs $\frac{d+20}{4}$ naira. Adding the pack of envelopes gives $d + \frac{d+20}{4}$ naira.

2. $2b + 3(b - 10) = 2b + 3b - 30 = 5b - 30$ naira.

   A ruler costs $b - 10$ naira. Two books cost $2b$, and three rulers cost $3(b - 10) = 3b - 30$. Total is $2b + 3b - 30 = 5b - 30$ naira.

3. One pencil costs $x - y$ naira, so 3 pencils cost $3(x - y) = 3x - 3y$ naira.

   Altogether the pen and pencil make $x$ naira, and the pen costs $y$ naira, so one pencil costs $x - y$ naira. Three pencils cost $3(x - y)$, which simplifies to $3x - 3y$ naira.
