## The revision pack

### The Order of Operations in Algebra

When you simplify an algebraic expression, you must work through it in a fixed order, not just left to right. The rule to remember is: **multiply and divide before you add and subtract**, and always deal with anything inside **brackets** first.

Here is how the order goes:

1. Work out anything inside **brackets** first.
2. Then do **multiplication** and **division** from left to right.
3. **Last** do **addition** and **subtraction** from left to right.

You already know how to add and subtract like terms, and how to multiply and divide them. This section is about putting those skills together in the right order so you get one correct answer.

**Example 1 — numbers first, to see the order**

Simplify (a) $18 - 6 \times 2$,  (b) $6 - 18 \div 3$,  (c) $6 \times 2 - 2 - 40 \div 4$.

**(a)** $18 - 6 \times 2$

Multiply before subtracting:
$$= 18 - 12$$
$$= 6$$

**(b)** $6 - 18 \div 3$

Divide before subtracting:
$$= 6 - 6$$
$$= 0$$

**(c)** $6 \times 2 - 2 - 40 \div 4$

Do the multiplication and division first:
$$= (6 \times 2) - 2 - (40 \div 4)$$
$$= 12 - 2 - 10$$
$$= 10 - 10$$
$$= 0$$

**Example 2 — the same order with letters**

Simplify (a) $3x \times 2 + 5x$,  (b) $17y - 5 \times 2y$.

**(a)** $3x \times 2 + 5x$

Multiply first:
$$= 6x + 5x$$

Then add the like terms:
$$= 11x$$

**(b)** $17y - 5 \times 2y$

Multiply first:
$$= 17y - 10y$$

Then subtract the like terms:
$$= 7y$$

**Example 3 — brackets come first**

Simplify (a) $7x \div x + 5$,  (b) $4x - (6 \div 3)$,  (c) $(24x \div 6) + x + (1 \times 5x)$.

**(a)** $7x \div x + 5$

Divide first. Since $7x \div x = 7$:
$$= 7 + 5$$
$$= 12$$

**(b)** $4x - (6 \div 3)$

Work out the bracket first: $6 \div 3 = 2$.
$$= 4x - 2$$

**(c)** $(24x \div 6) + x + (1 \times 5x)$

Do each bracket first. $24x \div 6 = 4x$, and $1 \times 5x = 5x$.
$$= 4x + x + 5x$$

Now add the like terms from left to right:
$$= 5x + 5x$$
$$= 10x$$

**Practice**

2. Simplify $32 + 8x \div 2$.

3. Simplify $3 \times 5x + 4x \div 2$.

4. Simplify $36y \div 6 + y + 2 \times 4y$.

### Removing Brackets and Simplifying

When an algebraic expression has brackets in it, you can often make it simpler by removing them. Removing a bracket means writing the terms inside it without the bracket, but the sign right before the bracket tells you what to do.

Keep these hints in mind:

- If a bracket has terms inside it, see whether you can simplify those terms first.
- A $+$ sign in front of a bracket does not change the signs of the terms inside. When you remove the bracket, every term keeps its own sign.
- A $-$ sign in front of a bracket changes every sign inside it: each $+$ becomes $-$ and each $-$ becomes $+$.

After removing the brackets, collect the like terms together and add or subtract them as you already know how.

Let us see the effect of a $+$ sign and a $-$ sign in front of a bracket.

Simplify:
(a) $a + (b + c)$
(b) $a + (b - c)$
(c) $2a - (3b - c)$

Solution

(a) The $+$ before the bracket leaves the signs alone, so
$$a + (b + c) = a + b + c.$$

(b) Again a $+$ sits before the bracket, so the signs stay the same:
$$a + (b - c) = a + b - c.$$

(c) Here a $-$ sign is before the bracket, so every sign inside changes: $3b$ is positive inside, and it becomes $-3b$; $c$ is negative inside, and it becomes $+c$. So
$$2a - (3b - c) = 2a - 3b + c.$$

Now remove the brackets and then collect like terms.

Simplify:
(a) $a + (a - b)$
(b) $6x + (3 - x)$
(c) $(3a + 2) + (5a + a)$

Solution

(a) Remove the bracket (the $+$ keeps the signs), then collect the $a$ terms:
$$a + (a - b) = a + a - b = 2a - b.$$

(b) Remove the bracket, then gather the $x$ terms:
$$6x + (3 - x) = 6x + 3 - x = 6x - x + 3 = 5x + 3.$$

(c) Remove the bracket, then collect the $a$ terms:
$$(3a + 2) + (5a + a) = 3a + 2 + 5a + a = 3a + 5a + a + 2 = 9a + 2.$$

A $-$ sign before a bracket changes every sign inside it. Watch for this in each step.

Simplify:
(a) $5x - (3y + z)$
(b) $10d - (9c - 4d)$
(c) $(6x - y) - (4x - 5y)$

Solution

(a) The $-$ before the bracket changes $+3y$ into $-3y$ and $+z$ into $-z$:
$$5x - (3y + z) = 5x - 3y - z.$$

(b) The $-$ changes $+9c$ into $-9c$ and $-4d$ into $+4d$:
$$10d - (9c - 4d) = 10d - 9c + 4d = 10d + 4d - 9c = 14d - 9c.$$

(c) Remove the first bracket as it is (the $+$ in front of it changes nothing), and change the signs inside the second bracket because a $-$ stands before it:
$$(6x - y) - (4x - 5y) = 6x - y - 4x + 5y = 6x - 4x + 5y - y = 2x + 4y = 2(x + 2y).$$

2. Remove the brackets and simplify: $(8x - 2y) - (9x - 4y)$

3. Remove the brackets and simplify: $12 - (7 - 2f) - (3 - f)$

### Word Problems with Algebraic Expressions

A word problem often describes a quantity in words and asks you to write it with a letter, then combine the parts. The key is to let each letter stand for one thing, read the problem slowly to decide whether to add or subtract, and then simplify the expression you build.

Think of it like this: the problem names some objects (a pen, a rubber, the boys and girls in a class) and tells you how their costs or numbers are related. You turn each sentence into algebra, one piece at a time, and then put the pieces together. As you go, keep the naira sign (₦) with every cost, and when you are asked for a total, add all the parts and simplify by collecting like terms.

<strong>Example 1</strong>

A pen costs ₦P and a pencil costs ₦15 less than a pen.

(a) Find the cost of a pencil.
(b) Find the cost of a pen and a pencil.

<strong>Solution</strong>

A pencil costs 15 naira less than the pen, so take 15 away from P:

(a) Cost of a pencil = ₦(P − 15).

(b) A pen and a pencil together means we add the two costs:

Cost of pen + cost of pencil = ₦P + ₦(P − 15)
= ₦P + ₦P − ₦15
= ₦2P − ₦15
= ₦(2P − 15).

So a pen and a pencil together cost ₦(2P − 15).

<strong>Example 2</strong>

A pencil costs ₦x and a rubber costs ₦40 more than a pencil.

(a) Find the cost of a rubber.
(b) Find the cost of a pencil and a rubber.

<strong>Solution</strong>

A rubber costs 40 naira more than the pencil, so add 40 to x:

(a) Cost of a rubber = ₦(x + 40).

(b) Cost of a pencil and a rubber:

₦x + ₦(x + 40)
= ₦(x + x + 40)
= ₦(2x + 40).

So a pencil and a rubber together cost ₦(2x + 40).

<strong>Example 3</strong>

In a class there are x boys and y girls. This semester 5 boys join and 3 girls leave the class. How many students are now in the class?

<strong>Solution</strong>

Start with the total: total number of students = number of boys + number of girls.

Boys: 5 join, so the number of boys becomes x + 5.
Girls: 3 leave, so the number of girls becomes y − 3.

Total number = (x + 5) + (y − 3)
= x + 5 + y − 3
= x + y + 5 − 3
= x + y + 2.

So there are now x + y + 2 students in the class.

**Practice**

1. A pen costs ₦h and a crayon costs ₦20 less than a pen.

   (a) Find the cost of a crayon.
   (b) Find the cost of a crayon and a pen.

2. Envelopes cost ₦d per pack, and a notepad costs ₦20 more than a pack of envelopes. Find the total cost of a pack of envelopes and a notepad.

3. A pen and a pencil together cost ₦x. If a pen costs ₦y, find the cost of 3 pencils.

## Check your work

**The Order of Operations in Algebra**

1. $34n$

   Multiply first: $3n \times 10 = 30n$. Then add the like terms: $4n + 30n = 34n$.

2. $32 + 4x$

   Divide first: $8x \div 2 = 4x$. $32$ and $4x$ are not like terms, so they cannot be combined. The answer is $32 + 4x$.

3. $17x$

   Multiply and divide first: $3 \times 5x = 15x$ and $4x \div 2 = 2x$. Then add the like terms: $15x + 2x = 17x$.

4. $15y$

   Divide and multiply first: $36y \div 6 = 6y$ and $2 \times 4y = 8y$. Now add the like terms from left to right: $6y + y = 7y$, then $7y + 8y = 15y$.

**Removing Brackets and Simplifying**

1. $7a - 3$

   The $+$ before the bracket keeps the signs: $12a - 7 + 4 - 5a$. Collect like terms: $12a - 5a - 7 + 4 = 7a - 3$.

2. $-x + 2y$

   The first bracket keeps its signs; the $-$ before the second bracket changes $+9x$ to $-9x$ and $-4y$ to $+4y$. So $8x - 2y - 9x + 4y = 8x - 9x + 4y - 2y = -x + 2y$.

3. $3f + 2$

   Each $-$ before a bracket changes every sign inside. So $12 - 7 + 2f - 3 + f$. Collect the numbers and the $f$ terms: $12 - 7 - 3 + 2f + f = 2 + 3f = 3f + 2$.

4. $5a + 9$

   The $+$ before the bracket keeps the signs: $8a - 5 + 14 - 3a$. Collect like terms: $8a - 3a + 9 = 5a + 9$.

**Word Problems with Algebraic Expressions**

1. (a) ₦(h − 20); (b) ₦(2h − 20)

   A crayon costs 20 naira less than the pen, so cost of a crayon = ₦(h − 20). A crayon and a pen together = ₦(h − 20) + ₦h = ₦h − ₦20 + ₦h = ₦2h − ₦20 = ₦(2h − 20).

2. ₦(2d + 20)

   A pack of envelopes costs ₦d. A notepad costs 20 naira more, so it costs ₦(d + 20). Total = ₦d + ₦(d + 20) = ₦(d + d + 20) = ₦(2d + 20).

3. ₦3(x − y)

   Out of the total ₦x, the pen costs ₦y, so one pencil costs ₦x − ₦y = ₦(x − y). Three pencils cost ₦3 × (x − y) = ₦3(x − y).
