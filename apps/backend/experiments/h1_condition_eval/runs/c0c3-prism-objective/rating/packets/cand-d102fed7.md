## The revision pack

### Order of Operations in Algebra

When you simplify an algebraic expression, you cannot just work from left to right. You must follow the order of operations, which tells you which part to work out first.

The correct order is:

1. **Brackets** — work out anything inside them first.
2. **Multiplication and division** — do these from left to right.
3. **Addition and subtraction** — do these from left to right.

You already know how to add, subtract, multiply and divide algebraic terms. Order of operations tells you the order in which to apply those skills. In an expression like $3x \times 2 + 5x$, you must multiply before you add, so you deal with $3x \times 2$ first and only then combine the $x$ terms.

**Example 1.** Find the value of each expression by following the order of operations.

(a) $18 - 6 \times 2$

Multiplication comes before subtraction, so work out $6 \times 2$ first.

$18 - 6 \times 2 = 18 - 12 = 6$

(b) $6 - 18 \div 3$

Division comes before subtraction, so work out $18 \div 3$ first.

$6 - 18 \div 3 = 6 - 6 = 0$

(c) $6 \times 2 - 2 - 40 \div 4$

Deal with the multiplication and division first, then subtract from left to right.

$6 \times 2 - 2 - 40 \div 4 = 12 - 2 - 10 = 10 - 10 = 0$

**Example 2.** Simplify the following expressions.

(a) $3x \times 2 + 5x$

Multiply first: $3x \times 2 = 6x$. Then add the $x$ terms.

$3x \times 2 + 5x = 6x + 5x = 11x$

(b) $17y - 5 \times 2y$

Multiply first: $5 \times 2y = 10y$. Then subtract.

$17y - 5 \times 2y = 17y - 10y = 7y$

**Example 3.** Simplify the following expressions.

(a) $7x \div x + 5$

Division comes before addition. $\frac{7x}{x} = 7$, then add $5$.

$7x \div x + 5 = 7 + 5 = 12$

(b) $4x - (6 \div 3)$

Work out the bracket first: $6 \div 3 = 2$. Then subtract.

$4x - (6 \div 3) = 4x - 2$

(c) $(24x \div 6) + x + (1 \times 5x)$

Work out each bracket first: $24x \div 6 = 4x$ and $1 \times 5x = 5x$. Then add all the $x$ terms.

$(24x \div 6) + x + (1 \times 5x) = 4x + x + 5x = 5x + 5x = 10x$

**Practice**

3. Simplify: $3 \times 5x + 4x \div 2$

4. Simplify: $36y \div 6 + y + 2 \times 4y$

### Removing Brackets to Simplify Expressions

When an expression contains brackets, you can remove them and then collect the like terms. Three hints will guide you every time:

- Always try to simplify the terms inside the bracket first.
- A $+$ sign just before a bracket does **not** change the signs of the terms inside the bracket when the bracket is removed.
- A $-$ sign just before a bracket **changes every sign** inside the bracket when the bracket is removed: each $+$ becomes $-$ and each $-$ becomes $+$.

Here is what that looks like in the simplest case. In $a + (b + c)$, the plus before the bracket leaves the signs alone, so $a + (b + c) = a + b + c$. In $a + (b - c)$ the same rule applies: $a + (b - c) = a + b - c$.

But in $2a - (3b - c)$, the minus before the bracket flips both signs inside: $3b$ keeps its term but its sign becomes $-3b$, and $-c$ becomes $+c$. So $2a - (3b - c) = 2a - 3b + c$.

Simplify the following.

(a) $6x + (3 - x)$

The plus before the bracket leaves the signs alone, so
$6x + (3 - x) = 6x + 3 - x$.
Collect the like terms: $6x$ and $-x$ are both in $x$, so
$6x - x + 3 = 5x + 3$.

(b) $(3a + 2) + (5a + a)$

Remove the brackets without changing any signs:
$3a + 2 + 5a + a$.
Collect the like terms: $3a + 5a + a = 9a$, so the answer is
$9a + 2$.

(c) $10d - (9c - 4d)$

The minus before the bracket flips both signs inside: $-9c$ and $+4d$.
So $10d - 9c + 4d$.
Collect the like terms: $10d + 4d = 14d$, so
$14d - 9c$.

(d) $(6x - y) - (4x - 5y)$

Remove the first bracket as it is. The second bracket is preceded by a minus, so $4x$ becomes $-4x$ and $-5y$ becomes $+5y$:
$6x - y - 4x + 5y$.
Collect the like terms: $6x - 4x = 2x$ and $-y + 5y = 4y$, so
$2x + 4y$.

**Practice**

2. Simplify $(8x - 2y) - (9x - 4y)$.

3. Simplify $12 - (7 - 2f) - (3 - f)$.

4. Simplify $(8a - 5) + (14 - 3a)$.

### Word Problems with Algebraic Expressions

A word problem tells a short story, and your job is to turn that story into an algebraic expression. You do this by deciding which part of the story is your unknown (the letter), then writing the other costs or numbers in terms of it. You already know how to add, subtract, multiply and divide algebraic terms, and you know how to remove brackets and simplify. A word problem just asks you to use those skills on a story.

Here is the plan to follow for any word problem:

1. Read the story and decide which item the letter stands for.
2. Write that item's value using the letter.
3. Write every other item as the letter, plus or minus a number, depending on the words "more than" or "less than".
4. Add the items together when the problem asks for a total.
5. Simplify by collecting like terms and removing brackets.

Remember the language of the problems: "more than" means add, and "less than" means subtract. If a pencil costs 15 naira less than a pen, then the pencil is the pen's cost minus 15.

Let's work through a problem together.

A pen costs ₦P and a pencil costs 15 naira less than a pen.

(a) Find the cost of a pencil.
(b) Find the cost of a pen and a pencil.

We let the pen be the letter, so the cost of a pen is ₦P.

(a) The pencil costs 15 naira less than the pen, so
cost of a pencil = ₦(P − 15).

(b) The cost of a pen and a pencil is the two costs added together:
cost = ₦P + ₦(P − 15)
     = ₦P + ₦P − ₦15
     = ₦2P − ₦15
     = ₦(2P − 15).

So the pencil costs ₦(P − 15), and the pen together with the pencil costs ₦(2P − 15).

Here is a second example, this time with "more than".

A pencil costs ₦x and a rubber costs 40 naira more than a pencil.

(a) Find the cost of a rubber.
(b) Find the cost of a pencil and a rubber.

We let the pencil be the letter, so the cost of a pencil is ₦x.

(a) The rubber costs 40 naira more than the pencil, so
cost of a rubber = ₦(x + 40).

(b) The cost of a pencil and a rubber is the two costs added:
cost = ₦x + ₦(x + 40)
     = ₦(x + x + 40)
     = ₦(2x + 40).

So the rubber costs ₦(x + 40), and the pencil together with the rubber costs ₦(2x + 40).

Word problems are not only about money — the letter can stand for a number of people too.

In a class there are x boys and y girls last semester. This semester, 5 boys join and 3 girls leave the class. How many students are now in this class?

The total number of students is the number of boys plus the number of girls.

Number of boys now = x + 5 (5 boys joined).
Number of girls now = y − 3 (3 girls left).

Total number = x + 5 + y − 3
             = x + y + 5 − 3
             = x + y + 2.

So there are now x + y + 2 students in the class.

## Check your work

**Order of Operations in Algebra**

1. $34n$

   Multiply first: $3n \times 10 = 30n$. Then add: $4n + 30n = 34n$.

2. $32 + 4x$

   Divide first: $8x \div 2 = 4x$. Then add: $32 + 4x$. There are no $x$ terms to combine with $32$, so the answer is $32 + 4x$.

3. $17x$

   Multiply first: $3 \times 5x = 15x$. Divide next: $4x \div 2 = 2x$. Then add: $15x + 2x = 17x$.

4. $15y$

   Work out the division and multiplication first: $36y \div 6 = 6y$ and $2 \times 4y = 8y$. Then add all the $y$ terms from left to right: $6y + y + 8y = 7y + 8y = 15y$.

**Removing Brackets to Simplify Expressions**

1. $7a - 3$

   No signs change when the brackets are removed:
$12a - 7 + 4 - 5a$.
Collect the like terms: $12a - 5a = 7a$ and $-7 + 4 = -3$.
So the answer is $7a - 3$.

2. $-x + 2y$

   Remove the first bracket as it is. The second bracket is preceded by a minus, so $9x$ becomes $-9x$ and $-4y$ becomes $+4y$:
$8x - 2y - 9x + 4y$.
Collect the like terms: $8x - 9x = -x$ and $-2y + 4y = 2y$.
So the answer is $-x + 2y$.

3. $2 + 3f$

   Remove the first bracket: the minus before it flips $7$ to $-7$ and $-2f$ to $+2f$. Remove the second bracket: the minus flips $3$ to $-3$ and $-f$ to $+f$.
So we get $12 - 7 + 2f - 3 + f$.
Collect the number terms: $12 - 7 - 3 = 2$. Collect the $f$ terms: $2f + f = 3f$.
So the answer is $2 + 3f$.

4. $5a + 9$

   No signs change when the brackets are removed:
$8a - 5 + 14 - 3a$.
Collect the like terms: $8a - 3a = 5a$ and $-5 + 14 = 9$.
So the answer is $5a + 9$.

**Word Problems with Algebraic Expressions**

1. (a) cost of a crayon = ₦(h − 20). (b) cost of a crayon and a pen = ₦(2h − 20).

   The pen is the letter, so the pen costs ₦h. (a) The crayon costs 20 less than the pen, so it costs ₦(h − 20). (b) Add the two: ₦h + ₦(h − 20) = ₦h + ₦h − ₦20 = ₦(2h − 20).

2. ₦(5d + 20)/4

   A pack of envelopes costs ₦d. The 4 notepads together cost 20 naira more, so 4 notepads cost ₦(d + 20), which means one notepad costs ₦(d + 20)/4. Total for a pack of envelopes and one notepad = ₦d + ₦(d + 20)/4 = ₦(4d)/4 + ₦(d + 20)/4 = ₦(4d + d + 20)/4 = ₦(5d + 20)/4.

3. ₦3(x − y)

   The pen and pencil together cost x naira, and the pen costs y naira, so one pencil costs ₦(x − y). Three pencils therefore cost ₦3(x − y).
