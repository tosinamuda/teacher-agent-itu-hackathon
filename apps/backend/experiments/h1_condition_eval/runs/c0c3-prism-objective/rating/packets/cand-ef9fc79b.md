## The revision pack

### Getting the Order Right in Algebra

When an algebraic expression mixes several operations, you cannot work from left to right in any order you like. The result depends on the order in which you carry them out. So algebra follows one agreed set of rules.

Do the operations in this order:

1. <b>Brackets</b> first — work out what is inside them.
2. <b>Order / Of</b> — powers like ² or ³.
3. <b>Division and Multiplication</b> — from left to right.
4. <b>Addition and Subtraction</b> — from left to right.

A handy way to remember the order is <b>BODMAS</b>: Brackets, Of/Order, Division, Multiplication, Addition, Subtraction.

The trick in algebra is that you first deal with multiplication and division wherever they appear, before you do any adding or subtracting. Then you combine like terms. Let's see this in action.

<b>Worked example 1</b>

Simplify 4n + 3n × 10.

Here there are no brackets and no powers, so Division and Multiplication come first. The only multiplication is 3n × 10.

3n × 10 = 30n

So 4n + 3n × 10 = 4n + 30n.

Now do the addition: 4n + 30n = 34n.

<b>Answer: 34n</b>

Notice that if you had added 4n + 3n first (getting 7n) and then multiplied by 10, you would have got 70n — which is wrong. The multiplication must be done before the addition.

<b>Worked example 2</b>

Simplify 32 + 8x ÷ 2.

Again, there are no brackets. Division and Multiplication come before Addition and Subtraction. The only division is 8x ÷ 2.

8x ÷ 2 = 4x

So 32 + 8x ÷ 2 = 32 + 4x.

There is nothing more to add — 32 and 4x are not like terms, so the answer is left as 32 + 4x.

<b>Answer: 32 + 4x</b>

<b>Worked example 3</b>

Simplify 3 × 5x + 4x ÷ 2.

No brackets, no powers. Division and Multiplication come first, from left to right.

3 × 5x = 15x, and 4x ÷ 2 = 2x.

Now do the addition: 15x + 2x = 17x.

<b>Answer: 17x</b>

<b>Worked example 4</b>

Simplify 7 × 3a − (3a + 5a) × 2.

Brackets come first. Inside the bracket: 3a + 5a = 8a.

So the expression becomes 7 × 3a − 8a × 2.

Now Division and Multiplication come before the subtraction. Work from left to right:

7 × 3a = 21a, and 8a × 2 = 16a.

Now do the subtraction: 21a − 16a = 5a.

<b>Answer: 5a</b>

Now try these yourself. Work them out on paper first, then check your answers below. Remember to deal with brackets first, then multiplication and division, and only then addition and subtraction — combining like terms as you go.

### Removing and Adding Brackets

Brackets group terms together so that we know which parts of an expression belong together. When you want to simplify an expression that has brackets in it, the first thing to do is remove the brackets — and the sign just in front of a bracket decides how the terms inside come out.

Think of a bracket as a box. If a <strong>plus</strong> sign sits in front of the box, then every term inside the box keeps its own sign exactly as it is. You can lift the lid and write the terms down unchanged.

If a <strong>minus</strong> sign sits in front of the box, then the box is being subtracted from everything before it. To take the whole box away, every term inside must change sign: a plus becomes a minus, and a minus becomes a plus.

Once every bracket has been removed, you finish by collecting the like terms — the terms that carry the same letter.

<strong>Example 1:</strong> Simplify $(12a - 7) + (4 - 5a)$

There is a plus sign before the first bracket, so remove it unchanged: $12a - 7$.
There is also a plus sign before the second bracket, so remove it unchanged too: $4 - 5a$.

Put it all together:
$$12a - 7 + 4 - 5a$$

Now collect the like terms. The $a$-terms are $12a$ and $-5a$:
$$12a - 5a = 7a$$
The number terms are $-7$ and $+4$:
$$-7 + 4 = -3$$

So the answer is:
$$\boxed{7a - 3}$$

<strong>Example 2:</strong> Simplify $(8x - 2y) - (9x - 4y)$

The first bracket has a plus sign in front, so it comes out unchanged: $8x - 2y$.
The second bracket has a <strong>minus</strong> sign in front. Every term inside must change sign:
- $9x$ becomes $-9x$
- $-4y$ becomes $+4y$

So we write:
$$8x - 2y - 9x + 4y$$

Collect the like terms. The $x$-terms are $8x$ and $-9x$:
$$8x - 9x = -x$$
The $y$-terms are $-2y$ and $+4y$:
$$-2y + 4y = 2y$$

So the answer is:
$$\boxed{-x + 2y}$$

2. Simplify $(8a - 5) + (14 - 3a)$.

3. Simplify $(6p + 9) - (4p - 2)$.

### Turning Word Problems into Algebra

Word problems describe a situation in everyday words, and your job is to turn those words into an algebraic expression before you simplify it. To do that, look for two things: the unknown amount (the letter) and the relationship that connects the amounts.

Suppose a pen costs h naira and a crayon costs ₦20 less than a pen. The pen's cost is h, so the crayon costs h − 20. If the question asks for the cost of a crayon and a pen together, you add the two: h + (h − 20). Now simplify by collecting like terms: h + h − 20 = 2h − 20. So both items cost (2h − 20) naira.

The key steps are always the same:
1. Decide what the letter stands for (the unknown amount).
2. Write the other amount in terms of that letter, using the words that tell you the relationship — "less than" means subtract, "more than" means add, "twice" means multiply by 2.
3. Combine the expressions you have written and simplify by collecting like terms.

Envelopes cost d naira per pack. Four notepads cost ₦20 more than a pack of envelopes. Find the total cost of a pack of envelopes and a notepad.

Step 1 — Decide what the letter stands for. The pack of envelopes costs d naira.

Step 2 — Write the other amount. The notepads cost ₦20 more than a pack of envelopes, so they cost d + 20 naira. Since there are four notepads and they cost the same each, one notepad costs (d + 20) ÷ 4.

Wait — read the question carefully. It asks for the total cost of a pack of envelopes and a notepad, so you need one notepad, not the set of four. One notepad costs (d + 20) ÷ 4, which we write as (d + 20)/4.

Step 3 — Add and simplify. Total = d + (d + 20)/4.

To add these you need a common denominator of 4. Write d as 4d/4:
Total = 4d/4 + (d + 20)/4 = (4d + d + 20)/4 = (5d + 20)/4.

So the total cost of a pack of envelopes and one notepad is (5d + 20)/4 naira.

**Getting the Order Right in Algebra**

1. 15y

   No brackets. Division and multiplication come first:
36y ÷ 6 = 6y, and 2 × 4y = 8y.
The expression becomes 6y + y + 8y. All three are like terms, so add them: 6y + y + 8y = 15y.

2. 16x

   Multiplication first: 2 × 6x = 12x.
Then 4x + 12x = 16x.

3. 9a

   Multiply first: 5 × 3a = 15a, and 2 × 3a = 6a.
Then subtract: 15a − 6a = 9a.

4. 4y

   Brackets first: 2y + 4y = 6y.
Then multiply: 6y × 3 = 18y.
Then subtract: 18y − 14y = 4y.

**Removing and Adding Brackets**

1. $2 + 3f$

   The first term, $12$, has no bracket. The bracket $(7 - 2f)$ has a minus sign in front, so change every sign: $7$ becomes $-7$ and $-2f$ becomes $+2f$. The bracket $(3 - f)$ also has a minus sign in front, so $3$ becomes $-3$ and $-f$ becomes $+f$.

So we get:
$$12 - 7 + 2f - 3 + f$$

Collect the number terms: $12 - 7 - 3 = 2$.
Collect the $f$-terms: $2f + f = 3f$.

Answer: $2 + 3f$.

2. $5a + 9$

   Both brackets have a plus sign in front, so remove them unchanged:
$$8a - 5 + 14 - 3a$$

Collect the $a$-terms: $8a - 3a = 5a$.
Collect the number terms: $-5 + 14 = 9$.

Answer: $5a + 9$.

3. $2p + 11$

   The first bracket has a plus sign in front, so it comes out unchanged: $6p + 9$.
The second bracket has a minus sign in front, so change every sign: $4p$ becomes $-4p$ and $-2$ becomes $+2$.

So we get:
$$6p + 9 - 4p + 2$$

Collect the $p$-terms: $6p - 4p = 2p$.
Collect the number terms: $9 + 2 = 11$.

Answer: $2p + 11$.

**Turning Word Problems into Algebra**

1. (a) h − 20 naira. (b) (2h − 20) naira.

   The pen costs h. The crayon costs ₦20 less, so it costs h − 20. Together they cost h + (h − 20) = h + h − 20 = 2h − 20 naira.

2. 3(x − y) naira.

   The pen and pencil together cost x, and the pen costs y. So one pencil costs x − y naira. Three pencils cost 3 × (x − y) = 3(x − y) naira.

3. 3m marbles.

   Tunde has m. His sister has twice as many, so she has 2m. Together they have m + 2m = 3m marbles.
