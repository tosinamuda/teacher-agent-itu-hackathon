## The revision pack

### Order of Operations in Algebraic Expressions

When an algebraic expression mixes different operations, you cannot simply work from left to right. You must follow a fixed order. This order is often remembered as **BODMAS** (or BIDMAS):

- **B**rackets first
- **O**rder (powers) — though you won't need them here
- **D**ivision
- **M**ultiplication
- **A**ddition
- **S**ubtraction

Division and multiplication are done before addition and subtraction. So in an expression like $18 - 6 \times 2$, you must multiply first: $6 \times 2 = 12$, then subtract: $18 - 12 = 6$.

The same rules hold when the terms are algebraic. For example, in $17y - 5 \times 2y$, multiply first: $5 \times 2y = 10y$, then subtract: $17y - 10y = 7y$. You combine the like terms only after doing the multiplication or division that comes first.

**Example 1.** Find the value of each expression.

(a) $18 - 6 \times 2$

Do the multiplication first: $6 \times 2 = 12$.

$18 - 12 = 6$

(b) $6 - 18 \div 3$

Do the division first: $18 \div 3 = 6$.

$6 - 6 = 0$

(c) $6 \times 2 - 2 - 40 \div 4$

Do the multiplication and division first, in any order: $6 \times 2 = 12$ and $40 \div 4 = 10$.

$12 - 2 - 10 = 10 - 10 = 0$

**Example 2.** Simplify the following expressions.

(a) $3x \times 2 + 5x$

Do the multiplication first: $3x \times 2 = 6x$.

Now add the like terms: $6x + 5x = 11x$

(b) $17y - 5 \times 2y$

Do the multiplication first: $5 \times 2y = 10y$.

Now subtract the like terms: $17y - 10y = 7y$

**Example 3.** Simplify the following expressions.

(a) $7x \div x + 5$

Do the division first: $7x \div x = \frac{7x}{x} = 7$.

Now add: $7 + 5 = 12$

(b) $4x - (6 \div 3)$

Work inside the brackets first: $6 \div 3 = 2$.

Now subtract: $4x - 2$

(c) $(24x \div 6) + x + (1 \times 5x)$

Work inside each bracket first: $24x \div 6 = 4x$ and $1 \times 5x = 5x$.

Now combine the like terms: $4x + x + 5x = 5x + 5x = 10x$

4. Simplify: $36y \div 6 + y + 2 \times 4y$

### Removing and Simplifying Brackets in Algebraic Expressions

When an algebraic expression has brackets, you can often make it simpler by removing the brackets and then collecting the like terms. Before you do anything else, always try to simplify the terms *inside* the bracket first.

What happens when you remove a bracket depends on the sign that stands just before it:

- A $+$ sign before a bracket does **not** change the signs of the terms inside the bracket when you remove it.
- A $-$ sign before a bracket **changes every sign** inside the bracket: every $+$ becomes $-$ and every $-$ becomes $+$ when you remove the bracket.

So the bracket in $a + (b + c)$ comes after a $+$, and removing it leaves the signs untouched. The bracket in $2a - (3b - c)$ comes after a $-$ sign, so the $+$ before $3b$ becomes $-$ and the $-$ before $c$ becomes $+$.

**Example 1 — removing a bracket after a $+$ or a $-$ sign**

Simplify (a) $a + (b + c)$, (b) $a + (b - c)$, (c) $2a - (3b - c)$.

**Solution**

(a) The $+$ before the bracket leaves the signs unchanged, so
$$a + (b + c) = a + b + c.$$

(b) Again the $+$ before the bracket changes nothing:
$$a + (b - c) = a + b - c.$$

(c) The $-$ before the bracket changes every sign inside: $3b$ becomes $-3b$ and $-c$ becomes $+c$, so
$$2a - (3b - c) = 2a - 3b + c.$$

**Example 2 — removing brackets and collecting like terms**

Simplify (a) $a + (a - b)$, (b) $6x + (3 - x)$, (c) $(3a + 2) + (5a + a)$.

**Solution**

(a) Remove the bracket, keeping the signs (it follows a $+$):
$$a + (a - b) = a + a - b = 2a - b.$$

(b) Remove the bracket, then group the like terms together before adding:
$$6x + (3 - x) = 6x + 3 - x = 6x - x + 3 = 5x + 3.$$

(c) Two brackets, both after a $+$. Remove them both, then collect the $a$ terms:
$$(3a + 2) + (5a + a) = 3a + 2 + 5a + a = 3a + 5a + a + 2 = 9a + 2.$$

**Example 3 — removing a bracket after a $-$ sign**

Simplify (a) $5x - (3y + z)$, (b) $10d - (9c - 4d)$, (c) $(6x - y) - (4x - 5y)$.

**Solution**

(a) The $-$ before the bracket changes $3y$ to $-3y$ and $z$ to $-z$:
$$5x - (3y + z) = 5x - 3y - z.$$

(b) The $-$ changes $9c$ to $-9c$ and $-4d$ to $+4d$:
$$10d - (9c - 4d) = 10d - 9c + 4d = 10d + 4d - 9c = 14d - 9c.$$

(c) Here the second bracket follows a $-$ sign, so every sign inside it changes:
$$(6x - y) - (4x - 5y) = 6x - y - 4x + 5y = 6x - 4x + 5y - y = 2x + 4y = 2(x + 2y).$$

**Practice**

1. Simplify $(12a - 7) + (4 - 5a)$.

2. Simplify $(8x - 2y) - (9x - 4y)$.

3. Simplify $12 - (7 - 2f) - (3 - f)$.

4. Simplify $(8a - 5) + (14 - 3a)$.

### Word Problems with Algebraic Expressions

A word problem puts a story around an algebraic expression. Your job is to read the story, turn each sentence into part of the expression, and then simplify. Words like "less than" mean subtract, and words like "more than" mean add. Start by deciding what each letter stands for, write the expression for the thing asked about, then simplify it by collecting like terms.

**Example 1** — A pen costs ₦P and a pencil costs ₦15 less than a pen.

(a) Find the cost of a pencil.
(b) Find the cost of a pen and a pencil.

**Solution**

The pen costs ₦P.

(a) A pencil costs 15 naira less than the pen, so it costs ₦(P − 15).

(b) A pen and a pencil together cost:
₦P + ₦(P − 15)
= ₦P + ₦P − ₦15
= ₦2P − ₦15
= ₦(2P − 15).

**Example 2** — A pencil costs ₦x and a rubber costs ₦40 more than a pencil.

(a) Find the cost of a rubber.
(b) Find the cost of a pencil and a rubber.

**Solution**

The pencil costs ₦x.

(a) The rubber costs 40 naira more than the pencil, so it costs ₦(x + 40).

(b) A pencil and a rubber together cost:
₦x + ₦(x + 40)
= ₦x + ₦x + ₦40
= ₦(2x + 40).

**Example 3** — In a class there are x boys and y girls last semester. This semester, 5 boys join and 3 girls leave. How many students are now in the class?

**Solution**

Total number = number of boys + number of girls.

Number of boys now = x + 5 (5 join, so add).
Number of girls now = y − 3 (3 leave, so subtract).

Total number = x + 5 + y − 3
= x + y + 5 − 3
= x + y + 2.

So there are now x + y + 2 students in the class.

**Practice**

1. A pen costs ₦h and a crayon costs ₦20 less than a pen.
   (a) Find the cost of a crayon.
   (b) Find the cost of a crayon and a pen.

2. Envelopes cost ₦d per pack, and a notepad costs ₦20 more than a pack of envelopes. Find the total cost of a pack of envelopes and a notepad.

3. A pen and a pencil altogether cost ₦x. If a pen costs ₦y, find the cost of 3 pencils.

## Check your work

**Order of Operations in Algebraic Expressions**

1. $34n$

   Do the multiplication first: $3n \times 10 = 30n$. Now add the like terms: $4n + 30n = 34n$.

2. $32 + 4x$

   Do the division first: $8x \div 2 = 4x$. Now add: $32 + 4x$. Since $32$ is a number and $4x$ is a term in $x$, they are not like terms, so the answer stays $32 + 4x$.

3. $17x$

   Do the multiplication and division first: $3 \times 5x = 15x$ and $4x \div 2 = 2x$. Now add the like terms: $15x + 2x = 17x$.

4. $15y$

   Do the division and multiplication first: $36y \div 6 = 6y$ and $2 \times 4y = 8y$. Now add the like terms: $6y + y + 8y = 15y$.

5. $5a$

   Work inside the bracket first: $3a + 5a = 8a$. Now the expression is $7 \times 3a - 8a \times 2$. Do the multiplications: $7 \times 3a = 21a$ and $8a \times 2 = 16a$. Now subtract: $21a - 16a = 5a$.

**Removing and Simplifying Brackets in Algebraic Expressions**

1. $7a - 3$

   Remove both brackets (each follows a $+$, so the signs are unchanged): $12a - 7 + 4 - 5a$. Collect the like terms: $12a - 5a - 7 + 4 = 7a - 3$.

2. $-x + 2y$

   The second bracket follows a $-$ sign, so change every sign inside it: $8x - 2y - 9x + 4y$. Collect like terms: $8x - 9x - 2y + 4y = -x + 2y$.

3. $2 + 3f$

   The two brackets each follow a $-$ sign, so change every sign inside each: $12 - 7 + 2f - 3 + f$. Collect the number terms and the $f$ terms: $12 - 7 - 3 + 2f + f = 2 + 3f$.

4. $5a + 9$

   Both brackets follow a $+$, so remove them with signs unchanged: $8a - 5 + 14 - 3a$. Collect like terms: $8a - 3a - 5 + 14 = 5a + 9$.

**Word Problems with Algebraic Expressions**

1. (a) ₦(h − 20); (b) ₦h + ₦(h − 20) = ₦(2h − 20).

   The crayon costs 20 naira less than the pen, so it costs ₦(h − 20). A crayon and a pen together: ₦(h − 20) + ₦h = ₦(h − 20 + h) = ₦(2h − 20).

2. ₦(2d + 20)

   A notepad costs ₦(d + 20). Total = ₦d + ₦(d + 20) = ₦(d + d + 20) = ₦(2d + 20).

3. ₦(3x − 3y), or ₦3(x − y)

   One pencil costs the total minus the pen, which is ₦(x − y). So 3 pencils cost 3 × ₦(x − y) = ₦3(x − y) = ₦(3x − 3y).
