## The revision pack

### Order of Operations in Algebraic Expressions

When an algebraic expression contains more than one operation, you cannot just work from left to right. You must carry out the operations in a fixed order, called the order of operations. Deal with anything inside brackets first, then work out divisions and multiplications (working left to right), and finally carry out additions and subtractions (again left to right).

Look at the expression $18 - 6 \times 2$. The multiplication must be done before the subtraction. So $6 \times 2 = 12$, and then $18 - 12 = 6$. If you subtracted first you would wrongly get $12 \times 2 = 24$.

The same rules hold when the terms contain letters. In $3x \times 2 + 5x$, do the multiplication first: $3x \times 2 = 6x$, then add the like terms: $6x + 5x = 11x$. When a term is divided, like $7x \div x$, write it as $\frac{7x}{x}$ and cancel: $\frac{7x}{x} = 7$.

<strong>Example 1 — find the value</strong>

(a) $18 - 6 \times 2$

Do the multiplication first: $18 - 12$

$= 6$

(b) $6 - 18 \div 3$

Do the division first: $6 - 6$

$= 0$

(c) $6 \times 2 - 2 - 40 \div 4$

Work the multiplication and division first: $(6 \times 2) - 2 - (40 \div 4)$

$= 12 - 2 - 10$

Now subtract from left to right: $10 - 10$

$= 0$

<strong>Example 2 — simplify</strong>

(a) $3x \times 2 + 5x$

Multiply first: $3x \times 2 = 6x$

$= 6x + 5x$

Add the like terms: $= 11x$

(b) $17y - 5 \times 2y$

Multiply first: $5 \times 2y = 10y$

$= 17y - 10y$

Subtract the like terms: $= 7y$

<strong>Example 3 — simplify</strong>

(a) $7x \div x + 5$

Work the division first: $7x \div x = \frac{7x}{x} = 7$

$= 7 + 5$

$= 12$

(b) $4x - (6 \div 3)$

Work inside the bracket first: $6 \div 3 = 2$

$= 4x - 2$

(c) $(24x \div 6) + x + (1 \times 5x)$

Work inside each bracket: $24x \div 6 = 4x$ and $1 \times 5x = 5x$

$= 4x + x + 5x$

Add the like terms: $= 5x + 5x$

$= 10x$

### Removing Brackets and Simplifying Expressions

When an expression contains brackets, you remove them and then combine the like terms. Three hints will carry you through every example:

- Always try to simplify the terms inside the bracket first, if you can.
- A $+$ sign before a bracket does not change the signs of the terms inside the bracket when the bracket is removed. Every term inside keeps its sign.
- A $-$ sign before a bracket changes every $+$ inside to $-$ and every $-$ inside to $+$ when the bracket is removed.

Once the brackets are gone, you collect like terms exactly as you already know how to do for addition and subtraction of algebraic terms.

<strong>Example 1.</strong> Simplify (a) $a + (b + c)$, (b) $a + (b - c)$, (c) $2a - (3b - c)$.

(a) A $+$ sign sits before the bracket, so every term inside keeps its sign:
$$a + (b + c) = a + b + c.$$

(b) Again a $+$ sign sits before the bracket, so the $-$ inside stays as $-$:
$$a + (b - c) = a + b - c.$$

(c) Here a $-$ sign sits before the bracket, so the $3b$ stays, but the $-$ before $c$ turns into $+$:
$$2a - (3b - c) = 2a - 3b + c.$$

<strong>Example 2.</strong> Simplify (a) $a + (a - b)$, (b) $6x + (3 - x)$, (c) $(3a + 2) + (5a + a)$.

(a) The $+$ before the bracket keeps every sign, so you write out the terms and then collect like terms:
$$a + (a - b) = a + a - b.$$
Combining the two $a$ terms gives $2a - b$.

(b) Remove the bracket with the $+$ in front:
$$6x + (3 - x) = 6x + 3 - x.$$
Collect the $x$ terms together: $6x - x + 3$, which simplifies to $5x + 3$.

(c) Start by simplifying what is inside the bracket, since $5a$ and $a$ are like terms: $5a + a = 6a$. So
$$(3a + 2) + (5a + a) = 3a + 2 + 6a.$$
Collect the $a$ terms: $3a + 6a + 2$, which simplifies to $9a + 2$.

<strong>Example 3.</strong> Simplify (a) $5x - (3y + z)$, (b) $10d - (9c - 4d)$, (c) $(6x - y) - (4x - 5y)$.

(a) A $-$ sign sits before the bracket, so every sign inside flips: the $3y$ keeps its $+$ meaning as written, and the $+$ before $z$ turns into $-$:
$$5x - (3y + z) = 5x - 3y - z.$$
There are no like terms to combine, so this is already simplified.

(b) The $-$ before the bracket flips the signs inside: $9c$ stays, and the $-$ before $4d$ turns into $+$:
$$10d - (9c - 4d) = 10d - 9c + 4d.$$
Collect the $d$ terms: $10d + 4d - 9c$, which simplifies to $14d - 9c$.

(c) Remove both brackets, watching the signs. The first bracket is added, so it keeps its signs: $6x - y$. The second bracket is subtracted, so both signs inside flip: $4x$ becomes $-4x$ and $-5y$ becomes $+5y$:
$$(6x - y) - (4x - 5y) = 6x - y - 4x + 5y.$$
Collect like terms: $6x - 4x = 2x$ and $5y - y = 4y$, giving $2x + 4y$.

### Word Problems with Algebraic Expressions

Many algebra problems come to you written in words. Your job is to read the words carefully, decide which letter stands for which amount, and then write the situation as an algebraic expression before you simplify it.

Here are the two key moves.

<b>Step 1 — pick a letter for each unknown.</b> The problem usually tells you what a letter means. For example, "a pen cost ₦P" tells you P is the cost of a pen.

<b>Step 2 — turn the words into an expression.</b> Watch the little words that tell you to add or subtract:
- "15 naira less than a pen" means <b>subtract 15</b> from the pen's cost: ₦(P − 15).
- "40 naira more than a pencil" means <b>add 40</b> to the pencil's cost: ₦(x + 40).
- "5 boys join" means the number of boys <b>increases by 5</b>: x + 5.
- "3 girls leave" means the number of girls <b>decreases by 3</b>: y − 3.

Once the situation is written as an expression, simplify it the way you already know — add like terms and combine the numbers. Then put the ₦ sign back in front of the final answer.

Let's work through a few word problems step by step.

<b>Example 1</b>

A pen cost ₦P and a pencil cost 15 naira less than a pen.

(a) Find the cost of a pencil.
(b) Find the cost of a pen and a pencil.

<b>Solution</b>

The cost of a pen is ₦P.

(a) The pencil costs 15 naira <b>less</b> than the pen, so we subtract 15:
cost of a pencil = ₦(P − 15).

(b) A pen and a pencil together cost the pen plus the pencil:
= ₦P + ₦(P − 15)
= ₦P + ₦P − ₦15
= ₦2P − ₦15
= ₦(2P − 15).

So a pen and a pencil cost ₦(2P − 15).

<b>Example 2</b>

A pencil cost ₦x and a rubber cost 40 naira more than a pencil.

(a) Find the cost of a rubber.
(b) Find the cost of a pencil and a rubber.

<b>Solution</b>

The cost of a pencil is ₦x.

(a) The rubber costs 40 naira <b>more</b> than the pencil, so we add 40:
cost of a rubber = ₦(x + 40).

(b) A pencil and a rubber together:
= ₦x + ₦(x + 40)
= ₦(x + x + 40)
= ₦(2x + 40).

So a pencil and a rubber cost ₦(2x + 40).

<b>Example 3</b>

In a class, there are x boys and y girls last semester. This semester, 5 boys join and 3 girls leave the class. How many students are now in this class?

<b>Solution</b>

The total number of students is the number of boys plus the number of girls.

Number of boys = x + 5, because 5 boys join.
Number of girls = y − 3, because 3 girls leave.

Total = (x + 5) + (y − 3)
= x + 5 + y − 3
= x + y + 5 − 3
= x + y + 2.

So the class now has x + y + 2 students.

## Check your work

**Order of Operations in Algebraic Expressions**

1. $34n$

   Multiply first: $3n \times 10 = 30n$. Then $4n + 30n = 34n$.

2. $32 + 4x$

   Divide first: $8x \div 2 = 4x$. The 32 and $4x$ are not like terms, so the answer stays $32 + 4x$.

3. $17x$

   Work the multiplication and division first: $3 \times 5x = 15x$ and $4x \div 2 = 2x$. Then $15x + 2x = 17x$.

4. $15y$

   Work the division and multiplication first: $36y \div 6 = 6y$ and $2 \times 4y = 8y$. Then $6y + y + 8y = 15y$.

5. $5a$

   Work inside the bracket first: $3a + 5a = 8a$. Now the expression is $7 \times 3a - 8a \times 2$. Multiply: $7 \times 3a = 21a$ and $8a \times 2 = 16a$. Then $21a - 16a = 5a$.

**Removing Brackets and Simplifying Expressions**

1. $7a - 3$

   A $+$ sits before each bracket, so keep all the signs: $12a - 7 + 4 - 5a$. Collect the $a$ terms: $12a - 5a = 7a$, and the numbers: $-7 + 4 = -3$. So the answer is $7a - 3$.

2. $-x + 2y$

   The first bracket is added, so it keeps its signs: $8x - 2y$. The second bracket is subtracted, so both signs flip: $9x$ becomes $-9x$ and $-4y$ becomes $+4y$. So we have $8x - 2y - 9x + 4y$. Collect like terms: $8x - 9x = -x$ and $-2y + 4y = 2y$. The answer is $-x + 2y$.

3. $5a + 9$

   A $+$ sits before each bracket, so keep all the signs: $8a - 5 + 14 - 3a$. Collect the $a$ terms: $8a - 3a = 5a$, and the numbers: $-5 + 14 = 9$. So the answer is $5a + 9$.

4. $2 + 3f$ (or $3f + 2$)

   The first $-$ before a bracket flips signs inside $7 - 2f$, giving $12 - 7 + 2f$. The second $-$ before $3 - f$ flips both signs, giving $-3 + f$. Altogether: $12 - 7 + 2f - 3 + f$. Collect the numbers: $12 - 7 - 3 = 2$, and the $f$ terms: $2f + f = 3f$. So the answer is $2 + 3f$, which is the same as $3f + 2$.

**Word Problems with Algebraic Expressions**

1. (a) ₦(h − 20)
   (b) ₦(2h − 20)

   (a) A crayon costs 20 naira less than the pen, so cost of crayon = ₦(h − 20).
(b) Crayon and pen together = ₦(h − 20) + ₦h = ₦h − ₦20 + ₦h = ₦(2h − 20).

2. ₦(2d + 20)

   A pack of envelopes costs ₦d. The notepad costs 20 naira more, so it costs ₦(d + 20). Together: ₦d + ₦(d + 20) = ₦(d + d + 20) = ₦(2d + 20).

3. ₦3(x − y)

   Pen and pencil together cost ₦x, and the pen costs ₦y, so one pencil costs ₦(x − y). Three pencils cost 3 × ₦(x − y) = ₦3(x − y).
