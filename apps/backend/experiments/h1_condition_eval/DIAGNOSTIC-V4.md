# Diagnostic v4 — does C1 lose the plan in the coding of it?

**Status: run on 2026-08-23.** Results are in the section at the end and in
`runs/DIAGNOSTIC-V4-RESULTS.md`, generated by `diagnostic_v4.py` exactly as
predeclared below. The five gates the review set before any spend were met
before it, each with the file that meets it.

## The question

`clean-six` C1 − C0 on taught-lesson alignment is **positive, unstable and
unconfirmed**: +1.06, +0.78 and +0.61 across three judge passes on the same
packets, +0.17 on the worksheet run — 4/4 positive, never the same lessons,
significant once. The layer trace (`condition_trace/`) locates what C1 is
given: position, materials, prior knowledge, assessment, and the **Gagné
labels** derived from her activity narration — never the narration. Three
event labels and one evaluation question is a typical section's whole
pedagogical input.

The diagnostic asks one thing: *did replacing her words with their codes
discard information the model could have used?*

## Three arms, one variable apart

| arm | condition | context variant | what it isolates |
|---|---|---|---|
| **D0** | C0 | `v3` | baseline under the newly frozen contract |
| **D1** | C1 | `v3` | the frozen C1, same contract |
| **D2** | C1 | `v4-activities` | D1 plus her activity narration, with `sets_work` |

D1 − D0 is the existing comparison re-measured under one contract. D2 − D1 is
the diagnostic. D2 − D0 is what a revised C1 would be worth.

D0 must be regenerated, not reused from `clean-six`: the shared contract gained
the `activities` entry, and on the wire that changes the **system message of
every C0 unit** (`wire/`: 0/17 identical against the frozen contract, user
messages unchanged). A variant C1 compared against an old C0 would differ in
two things.

## Call accounting

Six lessons carry **17 generation units** (3 + 3 + 2 + 2 + 5 + 2). Each unit is
one compose loop, and on `clean-six` a loop averaged **2.6 LM turns**
(209/210 ended by `submit`; one hit the 16-turn cap).

| | per arm | three arms |
|---|---|---|
| packs (6 lessons × 3 draws) | 18 | 54 |
| unit generations (17 × 3) | 51 | 153 |
| LM calls (≈ 2.6 per unit) | ≈ 130 | ≈ 400 |
| judge calls, one pass | 18 | 54 |

Generation is gpt-oss-120b on Bedrock mantle. Judging is Sonnet 4.6 on
bedrock-runtime; the last full 72-candidate pass cost about $1.49, so one pass
over 54 candidates is about $1.10, and the review asks for **two** passes —
repeated judgments, not replications — so about **$2.20** to judge.

## Predeclared, before any output is seen

- **Endpoint:** taught-lesson alignment, D2 − D1, paired by lesson over the
  mean of three draws, Wilcoxon signed-rank, two-sided. n = 6 — the floor is
  .031, and this is a diagnostic, not a confirmatory test.
- **Non-inferiority:** D2 is not below D1 on objective-and-curriculum
  alignment, scope-and-correctness, or clarity by more than 0.5 — the measured
  judge noise on identical packets.
- **Overlap is read, not shown:** `evaluation_overlap` in `_key.json`. If
  normalised reuse correlates with the alignment score at |ρ| ≥ .5 in either
  arm, that is reported beside the endpoint, not instead of it.
- **The no-assessment ablation runs only if** one of these fires: reuse
  predicts the score as above; D2 still omits a curriculum target that C0
  covers (the trapezium pattern, 3/3 draws in `clean-six`); or a practice set
  is more than half her evaluation questions by the numbers-changed measure.

## What is not in this diagnostic, by design

- **The curriculum coverage invariant.** The contract already says the
  objective is not narrowed, and C1 dropped the trapezium in 3/3 draws
  regardless; a sentence is not the fix. A deterministic coverage check is a
  common-harness change, and it regenerates every condition it touches.
- **Per-component provenance** on the composition tools. Same reason.
- **A planning stage.** Added to C1 alone it confounds input with pipeline;
  added to all, it is a new harness.

Each is worth doing and each comes after, so that this run changes one thing.

## What the result will mean

C2 will still be the condition with the note; D2 will not have worked examples
from the lesson, because the plan does not contain them. Her Presentation
narration across 17 core steps is 2,381 characters, and 7 of 17 name any
mathematics at all.

If D2 > D1: abstract event coding discarded usable lesson-plan information,
and the C1 definition should carry her words.

If D2 ≈ D1: in this six-lesson, one-teacher corpus, the plan's activity
narration adds little constraint beyond the objective, and the substance the
pack needs enters with the note at C2. That is a finding about this corpus and
this documentation convention, not about lesson plans as a class.

Either way `clean-six` stays what it is: the frozen result, under the frozen
definition, on the old rating surface. This does not replace it.

## Results (2026-08-23)

Full tables: `runs/DIAGNOSTIC-V4-RESULTS.md`. Runs `diag-v3` (D0, D1) and
`diag-v4` (D2); 54 packs, 153 unit generations, zero duds or redraws; two
Sonnet 4.6 passes per candidate, archived as `judgments_pass_a/b.json`.

### The diagnostic: D2 − D1 = **+0.00**

Her activity narration, sent beside the Gagné labels with `sets_work` and a
contract entry, changed the taught-lesson alignment score on **no lesson**. Pass
A: 0/6/0 — six ties. Pass B: 1/4/1, mean 0.00. Pooled: 1/4/1, p = 1.0. D2 is
non-inferior to D1 on every other dimension (largest shift −0.19 on scope,
margin 0.5).

That zero is a real zero. The judge's own repeat noise on identical packets was
5/36 and 1/18 candidates moved, mean |Δ| 0.14 — an instrument that would have
resolved a half-point effect.

**Reading, as predeclared:** abstract event coding did not discard usable
lesson-plan information. In this six-lesson, one-teacher corpus the plan's
activity narration adds no constraint the judge can see beyond what the labels
already carried, and the substance a pack needs enters with the note at C2. A
finding about this corpus and its documentation convention, not about lesson
plans as a class.

### D1 − D0 = +0.75 (5/1/0, p = .062 pooled)

The fifth and sixth judge passes on the C1−C0 contrast, under a new contract
and a new rating protocol, say what the first four said: positive, carried by
t2-w09 (+2.67), four lessons within ±0.67, t3-w06 at 0, not significant at
n = 6. *Positive, unstable, unconfirmed* stands.

### The overlap trigger fired — on a between-lesson confound

Raw Spearman ρ between normalised reuse of her evaluation questions and the
alignment score: +0.57 / +0.38 (D1), +0.60 / +0.58 (D2) — over the
predeclared 0.5 in three of four cells, so by the rule the no-assessment
ablation is warranted.

Post hoc, and labelled so: lesson-demeaned ρ is −0.05, −0.17, −0.03, 0.00.
The raw correlation is t2-w09 — the one lesson where she set work on every
step, reuse 3.0, score 4.67, and the +2.67 that carries D1 − D0. The richest
plan both reuses the most and scores the highest; within any lesson, reusing
more does not raise the score. Whether that is "copying is rewarded" or "the
richest plan gives the best pack" is exactly what the ablation separates, and
the rule says to run it. It is not run here: it is spend beyond what was
approved, named in the handoff.

### The other two triggers

- Trapezium: D0 3/3, **D1 3/3**, D2 2/3. `clean-six` C1 dropped it 3/3 under
  a contract that differs from D1's only by an entry that does not apply under
  v3 — so the omission moves with prompt bytes that do not reach it, and the
  earlier claim that it was a systematic input effect is withdrawn. D2's 1/3
  is a weak fire, reported as that.
- A practice set more than half her evaluation: no pack, any arm.

### What changes, and what does not

`clean-six` is unchanged: the frozen result under the frozen definition on the
old surface. The C1 definition does not need her narration; `v4-activities`
stays a named variant and does not become C1. The next thing that could move
C1 − C0 is not in the plan's representation — it is the ablation, and after
it, provenance and the coverage invariant.

## Ablation — predeclared before generation (2026-08-23)

The overlap trigger fired, so the rule runs this. **D3** = C1 under
`v4-activities-no-assessment`: D2 with her evaluation questions withheld from
the input and nothing else — the work she set in the steps stays. On the wire
(`wire/v4-activities--vs--v4-activities-no-assessment.json`): a pure removal of
`assessment_points` in the 10 C1 units that carried it, identical in the 7
that did not, C0 untouched. Run `diag-v4-noassess`, 18 packs, two judge
passes, same protocol.

- **Endpoint:** taught-lesson alignment, D3 − D2, paired by lesson over the mean
  of draws, Wilcoxon two-sided. The rating reference still carries her
  evaluation questions on every surface; only the generator's input changes.
- **Reading, fixed now.** Judge repeat noise on this protocol is ~0.14.
  - |D3 − D2| < 0.25: reuse did not earn the score. The t2-w09 effect is the
    plan, not the copying, and the overlap correlation was what its lesson-
    demeaned form said it was.
  - D3 − D2 ≤ −0.5: her evaluation questions contribute to judged alignment.
    That is legitimate by the rubric guidance *and* indistinguishable, at this
    design, from reuse being rewarded — both readings are reported, neither is
    chosen.
  - Anything between: reported as between, with the per-lesson diffs.
- **Also read:** t2-w09's D3 − D0 on its own — whether the one large lesson
  effect survives without assessment; D3's `evaluation_overlap` in the key
  (expected near zero, since the questions are no longer in the input);
  non-inferiority of D3 against D2 on the other three dimensions, margin 0.5.
- **Not read from it:** anything about C1's definition. D3 is an ablation.

### Ablation result (2026-08-23)

`diag-v4-noassess`: 18 packs, zero duds, two judge passes; `evaluation_overlap`
in its key is 0/0/0, so the questions reached the generator by no other route.
Full tables in `runs/DIAGNOSTIC-V4-RESULTS.md`.

**D3 − D2 = −0.28** (1/2/3, p = .25) — **between the bands**, as the
predeclared reading provides for. Withholding her evaluation questions costs
about a quarter of a point on judged alignment on average: above the judge's
repeat noise (0.14), below the effect band (0.5).

The mean hides where it comes from. Four lessons move 0 to ±0.33. The two that
fall are the two where her evaluation was the largest share of the plan's
question-shaped input: **t3-w09 −1.00** (no work set on any step, assessment on
both) and **t2-w09 −0.50**. Where the plan carried its own set work, removing
the evaluation changed nothing.

**t2-w09 survives: +2.67 → +2.17 against D0.** The one large lesson effect is
mostly the plan, not the copying — that is what the lesson-demeaned ρ said, and
the ablation confirms it.

**D3 − D0 = +0.47** (3/2/1). C1 with neither her narration nor her evaluation
still sits above C0 on alignment in three lessons, ties two, loses one. The
lesson-plan effect is not only the assessment.

Non-inferior on every other dimension; scope −0.25 (4 lessons down) is the
largest shift and inside the margin.

**What it separates and what it does not.** Reuse was not the main thing
earning the score — the largest effect survives without it. Her evaluation
does contribute, and contributes most where it was most of what she wrote
down. Whether that contribution is assessment congruence legitimately credited
or verbatim reuse rewarded is, as predeclared, not separable at this design:
both readings stand. Separating them needs a reference-side change (rate with
the questions hidden from the *judge*), which is a different experiment.

## v7-full — predeclared before generation (2026-08-23)

Everything her plan holds that the frozen definition withholds, in one arm.
Three arms, from two runs: **E0** = C0 and **E1** = C1 from `full-v3`; **E2** =
C1 from `full-v7`. Six lessons, three draws, two Sonnet 4.6 passes. C0 is
generated once — it is byte-identical across the two variants on the wire —
and both C1 arms are paired against it.

E2 adds four things to E1 at once: `activities`, `step_content`, `step_title`,
and her drawing travelling with the assessment question that needs it. That is
deliberate. Each has its own variant on the chain and can be separated later;
what this run asks is whether the whole of her plan, represented as fully as it
can be, moves the endpoint at all. If it does not, no decomposition is owed.

**C0 changed and must not be compared across runs.** Two shared-prompt edits
landed since `diag-v3`: the codebook is rendered per condition, so C0 is no
longer told what a lesson plan's fields mean; and the sentence introducing
those fields moved out of the task. `full-v3`'s C0 is the baseline for both
contrasts here and for nothing earlier.

- **Endpoint:** taught-lesson alignment, E2 − E1, paired by lesson over the mean
  of draws, Wilcoxon two-sided, n = 6, floor p = .031.
- **Also read, and named now:** E1 − E0, the frozen contrast re-measured under
  the new baseline; E2 − E0, what a fully represented plan is worth against no
  plan; non-inferiority of E2 against E1 on the other three dimensions at the
  0.5 margin; and `evaluation_overlap` per candidate.
- **Reading, fixed before the numbers exist.** Judge repeat spread on this
  protocol has been 0.14 and, on t3-w02, exactly zero.
  - |E2 − E1| < 0.25: representing her plan more fully changes nothing the
    rubric sees. With `activities` already measured at +0.00 and `step_content`
    84% the same words, this is the outcome to expect.
  - E2 − E1 ≥ +0.5: the frozen C1 was under-representing the plan, and the
    definition should carry these fields.
  - Between: reported as between, with the per-lesson diffs.
- **The figure is the one narrow check.** t3-w02's item 8 is the only
  illustrated evaluation question in the corpus. Under E1 the model receives
  three of its four measurements and no arc, and every draw answers 15 against
  a true 32. Under E2 it receives the drawing. That item is read directly, not
  through the rubric, because one item cannot move a lesson mean.
- **Not read from this:** anything about C2 or the note. E2 is still a
  plan-only condition; the mathematics her note carries is not in it.
