## Verdict

Leader: **`compose`** at recall 1.000, ahead of `compose-v2` by **0.000**.

The margin a winner must clear is **0.667** — the larger of the movement these candidates showed across their own draws (0.667) and the published run-to-run median (0.094). **This margin does not clear it**, so the top of the table is a tie and no winner is named.

The full spread is `compose` over `packed` by **0.234**, which does NOT clear it either. Nothing in this table separates from anything else: the same harness on the same lesson moves recall further between draws than any two harnesses differ.

A dud is a draw that raised rather than returned a pack. A candidate with duds is
not merely behind; it is unreliable, and that is the count to read first.

## What this does not cover

No external framework is included, because none is installed in this project. The
comparison is between the DSPy harnesses already in use, and calling it a survey of
the field would overstate it.

The workspace readers are excluded: they need materialised note files, which is a
dataset shape rather than a harness dimension.
