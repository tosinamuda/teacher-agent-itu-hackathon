## Source: step-t3w02-02 — Step II — types of plane shapes, their properties, similarities and differences

#### Hints

Plane shapes are also known as two dimensional shapes (2-D shapes). They include: triangle,
rectangle, squares, parallelogram, trapezium, circle and other polygons.

#### Triangles

Isosceles: a single tick on each of the two slanted sides, an arc at each base angle. Scalene:
one, two and three ticks on the three sides, an arc at each vertex. Equilateral: a single tick
on each of the three sides, an arc at each vertex.

[drawing attached to this section as unlabelled]

| Isosceles | Scalene | Equilateral |
|---|---|---|
| (i) 2 adjacent side are equal | No side equal | 3 sides are equal |
| (ii) base angles are equal | No angle is equal to the other | All angles are equal ($60^\circ$ each) |
| (iii) one line of symmetry | No line of symmetry. | 3 lines of symmetry. |

#### Squares and Rectangles

Square: a right-angle mark at each corner, a single tick on each of the four sides. Rectangle: a
right-angle mark at each corner, a single tick on each of the two longer sides and a double tick
on each of the two shorter sides.

[drawing attached to this section as unlabelled]

| Squares | Rectangles |
|---|---|
| i) All sides are equal | 2 opposite sides are equal |
| (ii) 2 pairs of parallel sides | 2 pair of parallel sides. |
| (iii) All angles are equal ($90^\circ$) each. | All angles are equal ($90^\circ$) each. |
| (iv) Four lines of symmetry | Two lines of symmetry |
| (v) Diagonals bisect it at right angle. | Diagonals bisect it but not at right angle. |

#### Parallelograms and Trapezium

Parallelogram: a double arrow on each of the two horizontal sides, a single arrow on each of the
two slanted sides. Trapezium: a single arrow on each of the two parallel sides, and one diagonal
drawn in.

[drawing attached to this section as unlabelled]

| Parallelogram | Trapezium |
|---|---|
| i) 2 pairs of opposite side are equal and parallel | Only a pair of sides are parallel |
| (ii) opposite angles are equal | opposite angles are not equal |
| (iii) No line of symmetry | No line of symmetry |
| (iv) Diagonals divide it into equal triangles | Diagonal divide it into two unequal triangles. |

#### Polygons

A polygon is a closed shape that has three or more straight sides.

| Name of Polygon | No of sides | No of triangle ($n-2$) |
|---|---|---|
| Triangle | 3 | 1 |
| Quadrilateral | 4 | 2 |
| Pentagon | 5 | 3 |
| Hexagon | 6 | 4 |
| Heptagon | 7 | 5 |
| Octagon | 8 | 6 |
| Nonagon | 9 | 7 |
| Decagon | 10 | 8 |
| Hen decagon or undecagon | 11 | 9 |
| Dodecagon | 12 | 10 |

## Source: step-t3w02-03 — Step III — parts of a circle

#### Parts of a circle

Two labelled circles. The first has the diameter drawn across it and a radius from its centre to
the circumference, with the arc and the circumference labelled. The second has a chord near the
top with the segment above it shaded, a shaded sector below with its right-hand bounding line
labelled Radius, and a tangent touching it on the right.

[drawing attached to this section as unlabelled]

- **Arc:** This is a curved part of the circumference.
- **Radius:** This is any straight line joining the centre of the circle to the circumference.
- **Chord:** This is any straight line joining two point on the circumference of a circle.
- **Diameter:** Any chord that passes through the centre of the circle.

## Source: step-t3w02-04 — Step IV — perimeter of plane shapes

#### Perimeter

Perimeter is the distance round a shape.

**Worked examples**

##### 1. Calculate the perimeter of a square of side 15cm.

[drawing attached to this section as unlabelled]

**Solution**

Perimeter of a square $= L + L + L + L = 4L$

$= 15 + 15 + 15 + 15 = 4(15)$

$= 60\mathrm{cm}$

##### 2. Find the perimeter of a rectangle whose length 10cm and breath 7cm.

[drawing attached to this section as unlabelled]

**Solution**

Perimeter of rectangle $= L + b + L + b$

$= L + L + b + b$

$= 2L + 2b$

$= 2(L + B)$

$= 10\mathrm{cm} + 7\mathrm{cm} + 10\mathrm{cm} + 7\mathrm{cm}$

$= 10\mathrm{cm} + 10\mathrm{cm} + 7\mathrm{cm} + 7\mathrm{cm}$

$= 2(10) + 2(7)$

$= 2(10 + 7)$

$= 2(17)$

$= 34\mathrm{cm}$

##### 3. If the perimeter of a rectangle is 60cm and its length is 17cm. Calculate its breadth.

[drawing attached to this section as unlabelled]

**Solution**

Perimeter $= 60\mathrm{cm}$

$P = L + b + L + b$

$60 = 17\mathrm{cm} + b + 17\mathrm{cm} + b$

$60 = 17\mathrm{cm} + 17\mathrm{cm} + b + b$

$60\mathrm{cm} = 2(17\mathrm{cm}) + 2b$

$60\mathrm{cm} = 2(17\mathrm{cm} + b)$

$60\mathrm{cm} = 34\mathrm{cm} + 2b$

collect like terms.

$60\mathrm{cm} - 34\mathrm{cm} = 2b$

$2b = 26\mathrm{cm}$

Divide both side by 2

$\frac{2b}{2} = \frac{26\mathrm{cm}}{2}$

$b = 13\mathrm{cm}$

##### 4. Find the perimeter of the trapezium below.

[drawing attached to this section as unlabelled]

Perimeter of trapezium $= a + b + c + d$

$= 11\mathrm{cm} + 9\mathrm{cm} + 5\mathrm{cm} + 4\mathrm{cm}$

$= 29\mathrm{cm}$

##### 5. Find the perimeter of the parallelogram below.

[drawing attached to this section as unlabelled]

**Solution**

Perimeter of parallelogram $= a + a + b + b$

$= 2a + 2b$

$= 2(a + b)$

$= 2(9 + 5)$

$= 2(14)$

$= 28\mathrm{cm}$

##### 6. Find the perimeter of a regular hexagon sides 5.2cm.

**Solution.**

[drawing attached to this section as unlabelled]

Hexagon has 6 sides.

Perimeter $= 6 \times 5.2\mathrm{cm}$

$= 31.2\mathrm{cm}$

##### 7. Calculate the perimeter of circle of radius 7cm (use the value $3\frac{1}{7}$ for $\pi$)

**Solution**

[drawing attached to this section as unlabelled]

Perimeter of a circle $= 2\pi r$ or $\pi d$

$= 2 \times 3\frac{1}{7} \times 7\mathrm{cm}$

$= 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 7$

$= 2 \times 22 = 44\mathrm{cm}$

using $\pi d$.

Since $r = 7\mathrm{cm}$

$d = 2 \times r = 2 \times 7\mathrm{cm} = 14\mathrm{cm}$

$\frac{22}{7} \times 14\mathrm{cm}$

$= 22 \times 2$

$= 44\mathrm{cm}$

##### 8. A rope is wound 50 times round a cylinder of radius 25cm. How long is the rope? $\pi = 3.14$.

**Solution**

[drawing attached to this section as unlabelled]

length of the rope $=$ circumference.

Circumference $= 2\pi r$

$= 2 \times 3.14 \times 25$

$= 157\mathrm{cm}$

Total length of the rope $= 157\mathrm{cm} \times 50$

$= 7850\mathrm{cm}$.

##### 9. The circumference of a circle is 440cm. Find the radius $(\pi = \frac{22}{7})$

**Solution**

[drawing attached to this section as unlabelled]

Circumference $= 2\pi r$

$440 = 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times r$

$440 = \frac{44r}{7}$

cross multiply.

$440 \times 7 = 44r$

$3080 = 44r$

divide both side by $44r$

$\frac{44r}{44} = \frac{3080}{44}$

$r = 70\mathrm{cm}$

##### 10. Calculate the perimeter of the shape below.

[drawing attached to this section as unlabelled]

**Solution**

length of the curved part $= \frac{1}{4} \times 2\pi r$

$= \frac{1}{4} \times 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 4.9$

$= \frac{1}{4} \times \frac{44}{7} \times 4.9$

$= \frac{11}{7} \times \frac{49}{10}$

$= \frac{11 \times 7}{10}$

$= \frac{77}{10}$

$= 7.7\mathrm{cm}$

Perimeter of the shape $= 4.9 + 4.9 + 7.7$

$= 17.5\mathrm{cm}$
