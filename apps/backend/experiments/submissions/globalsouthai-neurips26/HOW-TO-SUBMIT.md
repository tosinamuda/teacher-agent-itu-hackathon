# Submitting to GlobalSouthAI @ NeurIPS 2026

**Deadline 2026-09-07 17:59 UTC. Portal hard-expires 18:29 UTC.**
Review is double-blind; `abstract.pdf` is already anonymised. Do not add names to the PDF.

---

## Step 0 — before anything else: the supervisor's OpenReview profile

The `authors` field only accepts people who **already have an OpenReview profile**. If your
supervisor has ever published at an OpenReview venue (ICLR, NeurIPS, ACL, TMLR...), a
profile probably already exists, often auto-created from DBLP.

1. Go to https://openreview.net and search their full name.
2. If a profile exists, note the exact spelling and the `~Name_1` handle.
3. If it does not, they must create one at https://openreview.net/signup and confirm the
   email. Do this FIRST — everything else takes five minutes, this is the only step that
   depends on another person.

If the profile cannot be ready in time, submit with yourself as sole author. A missing
co-author cannot be added after the deadline.

## Step 1 — log in and open the venue

https://openreview.net/group?id=NeurIPS.cc/2026/Workshop/GlobalSouthAI

Click the submission button for **GlobalSouthAI Submission**.

## Step 2 — fill the form

Values are in `openreview-fields.md`. Field by field:

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | Which Component Carries the Effect? Ablating an ITS-Inspired Generation Framework |
| **Authors** | You, then your supervisor. Each must resolve to an existing profile. |
| **Keywords** | Paste the keyword line from `openreview-fields.md`. |
| **TLDR** | Optional. Paste the TLDR (under 250 chars). |
| **Abstract** | Paste the plain-text abstract (3,678 chars, limit 5,000). Plain text, no LaTeX. |
| **PDF** | Upload `abstract.pdf`. One page, letter, anonymised. |

Two mandatory radio confirmations, both must be accepted:
- author emails shared with Program Chairs
- accepted submissions, with author names, released publicly after the conference

There is **no field for choosing abstract vs short paper** — the PDF's length is the only
signal, which is why it must stay at one page.

## Step 3 — before you hit submit

- [ ] PDF is 1 page
- [ ] PDF shows "Anonymous Author(s)", no names, no institution
- [ ] Abstract box is plain text and under 5,000 chars
- [ ] Both confirmations ticked
- [ ] Author list resolves to real profiles

## Step 4 — after submitting

Save the submission URL and forum ID. You can **edit a submission until the deadline**;
after 18:29 UTC you cannot.

## Reciprocal review — check this

The venue states: authors with prior papers accepted at relevant venues may be required to
nominate an eligible co-author as a reviewer (up to four reviews), and *"failure to fulfill
this requirement may result in desk rejection."*
Reviewer sign-up: https://forms.gle/GsdmHeGBqkP3tmZC7

## This does not affect Africa in AI (deadline 10 Sept, 11:59 PM AoE)

GlobalSouthAI is non-archival and its FAQ permits work under review elsewhere. Africa in AI
requires only that submissions "must not have appeared in another venue, proceeding or
journal" — and on 10 September this work will have appeared nowhere, since GlobalSouthAI
notification is 20 September and publication is after the December event.
