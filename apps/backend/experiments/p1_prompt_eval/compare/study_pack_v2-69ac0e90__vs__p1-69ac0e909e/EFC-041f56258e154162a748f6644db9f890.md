*explanation*

## Objective lop04

### Candidate A
#### Writing billions in digits and words

*explanation*

A **billion** is one thousand million. Since one million is written as 1,000,000 (REF‑1), a billion equals 1,000 × 1,000,000 = 1,000,000,000. To write a number that includes billions, place the billions digit(s) to the left of the comma that separates the millions, then write the remaining millions, thousands and units. For example, 2 billion 350 million 120 thousand 5 is written as **2,350,120,005**.

*worked_example*

**Worked example:** Convert **0.15 billion** to digits and its full name.

1. Recognise that 0.15 billion = 0.15 × 1,000,000,000.
2. Multiply: 0.15 × 1,000,000,000 = 150,000,000.
3. Digits: **150,000,000**.
4. Name: **one hundred fifty million** (because the result is less than one full billion, it is expressed in millions).

*Note:* A common slip is to try to write “one hundred fifty billion” instead of “one hundred fifty million”.
