name: making-flashcards
description: Use when creating flashcards for a lesson objective, for a learner to revise from. Write four to eight cards, each with a question or prompt on the front and a short answer on the back, and check every card against what the lesson taught.
---
Write the cards for one lesson objective at a time.

## What a card is

A card tests one thing. If a card needs "and" in its question, make it two cards.

- The front is a question or a prompt the learner can answer from memory.
- The back is the answer in one or two short sentences. Give the working when the answer is a
  calculation.
- Use the words the lesson used. Do not bring in a term the lesson did not teach.

## What to leave out

- Trick questions, and questions whose answer is "it depends".
- Two cards that ask the same thing in different words.
- Any mention of the teacher, the class, or where the card came from.
