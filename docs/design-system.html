Counting in millions lop01
A million is 1,000,000. When we count in millions we add 1,000,000 each time, for example 1,000,000; 2,000,000; 3,000,000 and so on. This helps us understand very large numbers quickly.
Counting in millions lop01
One million is written as 1,000,000 and can also be expressed in exponential form as 10⁶. The textbook explains that a million represents a thousand groups of a thousand (REF-1). This magnitude can be visualised by comparing it to real-world examples such as staying awake for more than 11½ days.
