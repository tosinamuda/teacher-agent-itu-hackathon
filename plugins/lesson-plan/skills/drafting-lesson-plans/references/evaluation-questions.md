# Write the evaluation and the assessments

## Check questions

- Write the check questions in the lesson objective they check, beside its core step. Every
  lesson objective has one or more.
- A check question asks what its objective teaches. An objective on adding fractions with a
  common denominator is checked with "3/8 + 2/8", not with "What is a fraction?".
- Give each question the answer expected. Hints are optional, in the order to give them.

## The evaluation step

- The evaluation's questions are the check questions. Do not write questions into the
  evaluation step itself: give it a title, its content (a short summary for the teacher), its
  minutes and its activities only.
- Do not name a worksheet, an answer key, response cards or anything "attached" in the
  evaluation's content or activities, unless the instructional materials list it. Write "The
  students answer the questions in their exercise books", not "The teacher hands out the
  worksheet".

## Assessments

- Assessments are questions set for after the lesson. They are optional.
- Align each one to the lesson's objectives. Give each the answer expected, and a rubric where
  marking needs one.
